## 1. Public facts

### 1.1 Postquam is currently present on Faire as a wholesale brand
Faire Spain currently has an active **POSTQUAM COSMETIC wholesale brand page**. The page explicitly presents Postquam as a wholesale supplier and invites retailers to unlock wholesale pricing. The public brand page shows a **€150 minimum order** at the time of verification.

Source: Faire Spain — POSTQUAM COSMETIC wholesale brand page
https://www.faire.com/es/brand/b_gxvaw8vhhr

### 1.2 The Veloce Slim BLDC 110,000 RPM hair dryer is directly listed for wholesale
Faire currently lists **SECADOR PROFESIONAL VELOCE SLIM BLDC IONIC PRO 110.000 RPM** under POSTQUAM COSMETIC as a wholesale product. The public listing confirms:
- professional BLDC motor
- 110,000 rpm
- ionic technology
- self-cleaning function
- 4 heat modes
- 3 speeds
- 328 g without cable
- nozzle + diffuser included

The listing is explicitly marked as a wholesale product for retail stores and requires access to wholesale pricing.

Source: Faire — Postquam Veloce Slim BLDC Ionic Pro 110,000 RPM wholesale listing
https://www.faire.com/es-es/product/p_q3zvkmcbwy

### 1.3 Faire publicly describes its retailer buying conditions
Faire Spain currently states that retailers can access more than 100,000 brands and publicly advertises:
- low or no minimum orders for many brands
- up to 60 days to pay invoices without interest (subject to Faire eligibility/terms)
- free returns on a retailer’s first order from each brand

These are **Faire platform terms**, not Postquam-specific commercial terms unless explicitly shown on the Postquam listing.

Source: Faire Spain homepage
https://www.faire.com/es/

## 2. Channel structure verified from public evidence

A currently observable route is:

**Postquam Cosmetic → Faire B2B marketplace → independent retailer / professional reseller → end customer**

This is different from the previously verified route:

**Postquam-related product → The Beauty Corner third-party seller → Carrefour.es Marketplace → consumer**

The new evidence therefore adds a genuine **upstream wholesale layer** to the Postquam channel map.

## 3. Why this matters for 001 AI hair dryer

### Public-fact implication
A Spanish professional-beauty supplier with a current 110,000 RPM BLDC hair dryer is not only using consumer retail / marketplace distribution; it is also offering product through a **B2B wholesale marketplace accessible to independent retailers**.

### Inference — not a public entry rule
For 001, this suggests that a possible market-validation route may exist through B2B wholesale marketplaces such as Faire before or alongside direct negotiations with major chains.

This remains an **inference**. It does **not** prove that Faire will accept 001, that Spanish retailers will buy it, or that the same commercial conditions shown for Postquam will apply to a new Chinese brand.

## 4. Data governance / cautions

- The €150 minimum order is the public Postquam brand-page value observed on Faire at verification time; it may change.
- Faire’s general 60-day payment and first-order return terms are platform-level offers and may depend on retailer eligibility and market-specific terms.
- Wholesale pricing itself is gated and was not treated as public fact.
- No conclusion is made about Postquam’s exact importer-of-record structure for every electrical SKU.
- No conclusion is made about whether Postquam manufactures or sources the Veloce dryer.
