## 1. Channel identity

**Public facts**

- Perfect Beauty, S.L. is a Spanish company whose registered business activity includes the import, export and wholesale distribution of hairdressing, perfumery and beauty products, including electrical appliances and machinery.
- The company was incorporated in 2007 and is registered under CNAE 4645 (wholesale of perfumery and cosmetic products).
- Perfect Beauty's own website positions the company around professional hairdressing and beauty electrical equipment, hair care, skin care and professional accessories.
- Professional-industry directory BeautyMarket describes Perfect Beauty as a manufacturer and distributor of electrical appliances for hairdressing and cosmetics, serving professional hairdressing and beauty users.

**Channel classification**

- Primary: Vertical professional channel — hairdressing / beauty electrical appliances
- Secondary: Brand owner / importer-exporter / wholesale distribution
- Geography: Spain; downstream distributor network evidence exists nationally

## 3. Distribution role and downstream structure

**Public facts**

- BeautyMarket lists Perfect Beauty as a manufacturer/distributor of professional electrical equipment for hairdressing and cosmetics.
- Perfect Beauty's registered corporate object includes import/export and wholesale activity.
- Independent professional distributors publicly identify themselves as official Perfect Beauty distributors. Examples include Dubal Cosmetics and Beauty Mania.
- Beauty Cluster's 2021 profile of Dismay states that Dismay held exclusive national or territorial distribution rights for several brands, including Perfect Beauty at that time.

**Inference — not a public fact**

The available evidence supports modelling Perfect Beauty as more than a simple D2C brand: it participates in a professional distribution ecosystem in which specialist wholesalers/distributors resell Perfect Beauty products to salons and professional customers.

Do **not** infer from public evidence that Dismay remains the exclusive national Perfect Beauty distributor in 2026; the cited exclusivity evidence is historical (2021) and should be revalidated.
