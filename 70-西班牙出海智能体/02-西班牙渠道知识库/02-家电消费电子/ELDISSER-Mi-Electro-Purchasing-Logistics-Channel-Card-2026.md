# ELDISSER × Mi Electro — Purchasing / Logistics Channel Card (2026)

> Status: PUBLIC FACTS + TO VERIFY only
> Updated: 2026-08-31
> Leon View: NONE added or modified

## 1. Channel identity

- **ELDISSER** presents itself as a Spanish electro-appliance distribution group serving associated retailers.
- **Mi Electro** is the consumer-facing omnichannel retail network associated with ELDISSER.
- Public industry evidence continues to classify retailers as `ELDISSER MI ELECTRO (C.C. ELECTRO) - ASOCIADO PLATAFORMA`.

## 2. Current public scale — source-scope warning

### ELDISSER official group figures

ELDISSER's current official website states:

- More than **50 years** in appliance distribution.
- More than **€200 million turnover**.
- **33,000 m² warehouse**.
- More than **350 associated stores**.

The same page also contains a separate marketing line describing the chain as having **more than 300 stores**.

### Mi Electro official retail-network figures

Mi Electro's current official website states:

- **500 Mi Electro stores** / more than **500 physical points of sale**.
- More than **65,000 m²** in its "Sobre nosotros" page.
- Consumer orders placed online are assigned to a nearby Mi Electro store; the serving store receives the product margin / service revenue according to Mi Electro's public explanation.

### Data-governance rule

**Do not treat `350+ ELDISSER associated stores` and `500+ Mi Electro stores/points of sale` as the same denominator.** Both are current first-party claims, but the public pages do not explain the scope reconciliation. Until a higher-quality source or company clarification resolves it:

- ELDISSER platform scale = `350+ associated stores` (official ELDISSER scope)
- Mi Electro retail-network scale = `500+ stores / physical POS` (official Mi Electro scope)
- Relationship between the two counts = **TO VERIFY**

Do not mechanically overwrite one with the other and do not add them together.

## 3. Association / entry proposition published by ELDISSER

ELDISSER publicly advertises the following conditions to prospective associated retailers:

- No association fees / quotas / canon.
- No advertising cost.
- Product delivery in **24–48 hours**.
- Access to all product ranges / leading brands and group purchasing tariffs.
- Mi Electro exterior branding and support for interior adaptation / PLV.
- Customer financing and management software.
- Monthly Mi Electro promotional leaflet and merchandising.
- No stock cost and no transport cost (as presented in ELDISSER's association proposition).
- Purchase-volume bonuses depending on product ranges and brands.
- Payment-condition support.

These are **retailer-association terms**, not supplier onboarding conditions.

## 4. Logistics / omnichannel operating facts

Mi Electro states publicly that:

- Online orders are assigned to the store nearest the customer.
- Trucks leave for stores every day.
- The nearby store can serve the customer the next day for eligible orders.
- The store that fulfills the online purchase receives the economic benefit/margin from that order and associated service charges.

This supports classifying Mi Electro as a genuine store-linked omnichannel model rather than a pure centralized ecommerce storefront.

A 2026 Alimarket logistics listing also identifies an ELDISSER electro/technology distribution logistics platform at **P.I. Villalonquéjar, Burgos**. Exact role, ownership, warehouse size and relationship to the 33,000 m² official group figure remain **TO VERIFY**.

## 5. 2026 network expansion evidence

Alimarket reported on **2026-04-20** that ELDISSER had incorporated **14 new stores** in 2026 up to that point, mostly Mi Electro (with some Activa), totaling approximately **3,575 m²** of sales area. Reported expansion areas included Aragón, Castilla y León, La Rioja, Andalucía and Cataluña.

This is current operational evidence that the network was still actively recruiting / identifying stores in 2026.

## 6. 001 AI hair-dryer relevance — verified assortment facts

Mi Electro's current public assortment includes products directly relevant to the 001 high-speed hair-dryer benchmark, including:

- **Taurus Digital Air Force AI** — official Mi Electro product page states **120,000 rpm**, 1,100 W, 165 km/h airflow, Ultra Ionic Care and 10 speed/temperature combinations.
- **Cecotec Bamba CeramicCare** hair styler — **110,000 rpm brushless** motor, Coanda function, negative ions and multiple heads.
- **Jata Marco Aldany JBSC5577** — Mi Electro product data states **110,000 rpm**, ionic function, 2,000 W, diffuser and concentrator.
- Mi Electro also lists Laifen Swift SE variants, confirming presence of a specialist high-speed hair-care brand in assortment, although the retrieved Mi Electro page did not expose motor-speed data and no additional parameter is inferred here.

### What this proves

Public fact: high-speed 110k/120k personal-care appliances are already accepted as product types in the Mi Electro assortment.

### What this does NOT prove

It does **not** prove:

- ELDISSER centrally bought every listed SKU.
- Who is the supplier / importer of record for each SKU.
- Who owns inventory.
- Whether a new supplier can enter through central ELDISSER purchasing, a regional platform, individual stores, or another route.
- MOQ, listing fee, rebates, payment terms, sell-through guarantees, RAEE/EPR allocation or SAT obligations.

All of the above remain **TO VERIFY**.
