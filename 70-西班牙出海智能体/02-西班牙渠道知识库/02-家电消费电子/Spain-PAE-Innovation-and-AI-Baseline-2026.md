## 2. Public facts

### 2.1 Innovation themes in Spanish PAE

Alimarket's 11 May 2026 report on innovation in the Spanish PAE market states that the category is increasingly incorporating technology, connectivity and AI. The report identifies several broad directions in Spain:

- hyper-personalisation;
- very high energy efficiency;
- more compact products;
- multifunction / multitask products;
- more experiential products integrated into consumer lifestyle;
- innovation aimed at faster, easier and more effective task completion.

The report also states that the price premium associated with R&D tends to become more democratised after the first year, and notes that innovation decisions in Spain are influenced by small household size and an ageing population.

Participating manufacturers cited by Alimarket include Cecotec, Taurus, Ufesa, Jata, River International, Spectrum Brands, Dreame, Versuni and others.

Source:

## 3. AI Analysis — not Leon View

For 001 AI Hair Dryer, the market signal is that "AI" is becoming part of a broader PAE innovation vocabulary, but AI alone is unlikely to remain a durable premium justification.

A stronger differentiation test should ask what the AI capability actually changes for the user, for example:

- automatic temperature / airflow adjustment;
- user or hair-state adaptation;
- attachment recognition;
- thermal-damage reduction;
- energy optimisation;
- lower cognitive effort / easier use;
- measurable improvement in drying or styling outcome.

The Alimarket observation that innovation premiums tend to democratise after the first year is especially relevant to 001: a technical feature that initially supports premium pricing may quickly migrate into lower price bands.

This suggests that channel and product analysis should separate:

1. Feature novelty;
2. User-visible benefit;
3. Time-to-commoditisation;
4. Price premium sustainability;
5. Channel margin sustainability.
