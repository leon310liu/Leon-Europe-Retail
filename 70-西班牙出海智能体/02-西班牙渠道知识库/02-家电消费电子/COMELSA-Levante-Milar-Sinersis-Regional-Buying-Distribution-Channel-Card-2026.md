## 1. Public facts

### Company / channel role

- COMELSA (Comercializadora Electrodomésticos, S.A.) was created in 1981 after a group of Valencian appliance retailers associated to improve purchasing competitiveness.
- COMELSA describes itself as the **Milar appliance distribution center for Spain's Levante zone** and as a purchasing center operating inside Sinersis.
- Sinersis currently describes COMELSA as a leading purchasing center in appliance distribution with presence in Comunidad Valenciana, Albacete, Teruel, Murcia and Baleares.
- COMELSA operates with the **Milar** retail banner.

Sources:
- COMELSA official history: https://www.comelsa.es/historia-comelsa/
- COMELSA official home/services: https://www.comelsa.es/
- Sinersis associate profile: https://www.sinersis.es/asociado/comelsa/

## 2. Current scale and operating infrastructure

COMELSA's current official website provides several operational facts that are useful for channel modeling:

- **92 establishments** are stated on the current homepage.
- The store-locator page states **92 points of sale** in the COMELSA distribution network.
- Coverage: Comunidad Valenciana, Albacete, Teruel, Murcia and Baleares.
- **15,000 m² warehouse**.
- Warehouse-management system by locations and a **robotized warehouse**.
- **24-hour delivery routes to points of sale**.
- **15,000+ references available in real time**.
- **230 brands**.
- COMELSA says it has agreements with leading brands and financial entities.
- Its commercial/marketing team performs **direct negotiation with major suppliers for offers, campaigns and protocols**.
- It uses a proprietary POS-management environment (DIMILAR), portal/intranet between central office and stores, order/stock/invoice systems, and BI tools.

Source:
- https://www.comelsa.es/
- https://www.comelsa.es/encuentra-tu-tienda-milar-mas-cercana/

## 3. Procurement / distribution structure supported by public evidence

Public sources support the following factual structure:

**Suppliers / brands → COMELSA regional purchasing & distribution platform → Milar associated / owned stores → consumers**

Evidence:

1. COMELSA explicitly calls itself a **Central de Compras**.
2. COMELSA says its commercial/marketing function directly negotiates offers, campaigns and protocols with main suppliers.
3. COMELSA maintains a central logistics warehouse and delivers to points of sale within 24 hours.
4. Sinersis describes COMELSA as a purchasing center in appliance distribution.

This supports classification as a genuine regional purchasing/distribution node rather than merely a branding or marketing organization.

### Important boundary

The public information does **not** establish:

- whether all new SKUs require COMELSA approval;
- whether Sinersis framework agreements override or constrain COMELSA supplier decisions;
- whether individual Milar stores can independently list products outside the central assortment;
- whether COMELSA accepts direct supply from a China-based manufacturer without a Spanish/EU importer;
- MOQ, listing fees, rebates, payment terms, exclusivity or sell-through requirements.

All of the above remain **TO VERIFY**.

## 5. Data conflicts / freshness governance

### Store-count conflict

Current public sources are not internally consistent:

- COMELSA current homepage: **92 establishments**.
- COMELSA store-locator headline: **92 points of sale**.
- The same store-locator body text says COMELSA groups **more than one hundred Milar establishments**.
- COMELSA history page says **more than 100 Milar stores**.
- Sinersis associate profile says **more than 90 stores**.

Knowledge-base treatment:

- Use **92 points of sale / establishments** as the most specific current number displayed by COMELSA's current operational pages.
- Retain **>100** and **>90** as conflicting public statements.
- Mark exact current network size as **OPEN DATA CONFLICT / VERIFY BEFORE EXTERNAL USE**.

Sources:
- https://www.comelsa.es/
- https://www.comelsa.es/encuentra-tu-tienda-milar-mas-cercana/
- https://www.comelsa.es/historia-comelsa/
- https://www.sinersis.es/asociado/comelsa/

### Historical Sinersis scale data

COMELSA's history page still states that Sinersis manages approximately **2,500 points of sale** and references banners including Confort. This is not suitable as a 2026 current-scale fact.

Sinersis' current official homepage instead states:

- **1,500+ stores in Spain**;
- 17 regional associated platforms;
- banners: Tien21, Milar and Euronics.

Therefore the 2,500-store statement should be treated as a **historical/outdated website statement**, not current 2026 data.

Current Sinersis source:
- https://www.sinersis.es/
