## 1. Public facts — current structure

### 1.1 Role
- Grupo SEGESA Cadena Redder S.A. states that it was created in 1987 by appliance and consumer-electronics retailers to pool strength and obtain more competitive conditions.
- SEGESA currently describes itself as one of Europe’s major buying centres (`central de compras`).
- Its official “Quiénes somos” page currently states **12 grandes plataformas / socios**.

Source: SEGESA official, https://segesa.es/es/quienes-somos (verified 2026-08-28).

### 1.2 Partner / platform / warehouse counts must not be merged
SEGESA’s current official pages expose three distinct counts:
- **12 socios / grandes plataformas** on `Quiénes somos`;
- **13 storage and distribution platforms** on the partner page;
- **14 almacenes** around Spain on the Intranets/PIM page.

These are not necessarily contradictory: they may represent different operating units. The knowledge base must therefore keep `socios`, `plataformas logísticas` and `almacenes` as separate fields rather than using a single “platform count”.

Sources:
- https://segesa.es/es/quienes-somos
- https://segesa.es/en/our-partners
- https://segesa.es/es/intranets

## 2. 2026 membership transition audit

### 2.1 Medired moved to Sinersis
Electromarket reported on 19 December 2025 that Grupo Medired would join Sinersis effective **1 January 2026**. Sinersis currently hosts Medired as an associated group on its own website.

Sources:
- Electromarket, 2025-12-19: https://www.electromarket.com/noticia/32682/grupo-medired-pasara-a-formar-parte-de-sinersis-a-partir-del-proximo.html
- Sinersis current Medired page: https://www.sinersis.es/asociado/medired/

Data-governance rule: any knowledge-base statement that presents `Medired = SEGESA/Cadena Redder` as a current 2026 relationship is stale and should be treated as historical only unless a dated source explicitly describes the historical period.

### 2.2 Aseminfor joined SEGESA in 2026
Electromarket reported on **15 April 2026** that Aseminfor, a grouping of IT wholesalers founded in 2012, formally joined Grupo SEGESA Cadena Redder. SEGESA’s current official partner page now lists Aseminfor.

Sources:
- Electromarket, 2026-04-15: https://www.electromarket.com/noticia/33358/aseminfor-agrupacion-de-mayoristas-informaticos-se-suma-a-grupo-se.html
- SEGESA current partner page: https://segesa.es/en/our-partners

Public-fact conclusion: SEGESA’s membership structure changed materially around the 2025/2026 transition; current partner data should not be copied from older channel maps without date control.

## 4. 001 AI hair dryer relevance

### Public Fact
- SEGESA has a real, material **PAE** business: Electromarket reports **€28.09m PAE turnover in 2025**.
- SEGESA’s own current infrastructure explicitly includes `pequeño electrodoméstico` among its product categories and operates a PIM/product-range system.

### AI Analysis — not Leon View
The combination of a national specialist-retail network, regional buying/logistics structure, PAE turnover and shared product-information infrastructure makes SEGESA/Cadena Redder structurally relevant for 001 AI hair-dryer channel research.

However, public evidence reviewed in this run does **not** establish:
- who has final hair-dryer SKU approval (SEGESA central vs regional socios/platforms vs store);
- whether a new hair-care brand can run a regional pilot before national listing;
- commercial terms, MOQ, rebates, listing fees or payment terms;
- contract/invoice entity;
- importer-of-record responsibility;
- RII-AEE / RAEE, packaging EPR and SAT/repair responsibilities;
- the number of Cadena Redder stores that actively range hair dryers.

All of the above remain **To Verify**.

## 5. Knowledge-base taxonomy correction

### Public Fact
Electromarket Electroguía lists **HGM Master Cadena** separately from **SEGESA Cadena Redder**. For 2025 it reports HGM Master Cadena at 166 points of sale, while SEGESA Cadena Redder is reported at 1,244 points of sale.

Source: https://www.electromarket.com/revista/378/45/

### Governance rule
Do **not** classify `Master Cadena` as a SEGESA banner. In the current industry dataset it belongs to **HGM**, while SEGESA’s specialist banner is **Cadena Redder**. This distinction should be preserved in future Channel Cards and channel trees.
