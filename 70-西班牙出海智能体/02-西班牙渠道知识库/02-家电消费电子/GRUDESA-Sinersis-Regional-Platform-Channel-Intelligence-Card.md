## 2. Public Facts

### 2.1 Origin and procurement logic
Sinersis' current GRUDESA profile states that GRUDESA was created in 1990 by a group of small merchants in Murcia who joined together to carry out **joint purchasing in order to obtain better commercial conditions**. The same profile describes its role as serving points of sale across Murcia and nearby territories from its regional platform.

This is direct evidence that GRUDESA is not merely a logistics warehouse: collective purchasing is part of its original and publicly described business logic.

### 2.2 Regional scope and service portfolio
Sinersis' current profile says GRUDESA initially focused on appliances and subsequently expanded into IT, mobile telephony, appliance repair, air-conditioning installation, extended warranties and household goods.

Its operating scope is described as the entire Región de Murcia plus Cox, Vera and Nerpio, through points of sale and direct service from the platform.

### 2.3 Retail network evidence
Professional industry media reported in October 2022 that GRUDESA consisted of about **50 merchants and more than 65 stores** in Murcia and northern Almería. This is a historical industry figure and must not be presented as the current 2026 store count.

Alimarket reported on **13 June 2025** that GRUDESA/Tien21 planned to open another directly managed store after summer 2025, which would become its **fifth company-operated store** if completed. Publicly available article text does not, by itself, confirm completion of the opening; treat this as a 2025 expansion plan until separately verified.

### 2.4 Legal / activity classification
Current public business-directory records identify Grupo de Electrodomésticos Asociados, S.L. under **CNAE 4643 — wholesale of household electrical appliances**, headquartered in Murcia. These are secondary sources; legal data must be re-verified before contracting.

### 2.5 Supplier / category interaction signal
A 2022 professional-industry report documented a GRUDESA delegation visiting Grupo Svan's logistics facilities and learning about product novelties, after-sales infrastructure and spare-parts capabilities. This is historical evidence that the regional organization has direct interaction with appliance suppliers and evaluates not only products but also after-sales and logistics capability.

## 3. Data Quality / Conflicts

- **Store count:** the public figure of 50 merchants / 65+ stores dates from 2022 and is historical. Do not use it as a 2026 current network count without fresh confirmation.
- **Company-operated stores:** Alimarket's 2025 article describes a planned fifth own store; it is a plan, not proof that the opening was completed.
- **Geographic definitions:** Sinersis describes Murcia plus Cox, Vera and Nerpio; older industry coverage describes Murcia and northern Almería. Store-level mapping is still required.
- **Procurement structure:** collective purchasing is confirmed as part of GRUDESA's model, but current authority boundaries between Sinersis central framework negotiations, GRUDESA regional decisions and individual member-store choices are not yet public.

## 4. 001 AI Hair Dryer Relevance

### Publicly supported relevance
GRUDESA operates within the appliance / PAE ecosystem and its public scope includes services and categories adjacent to Personal Care. Its Sinersis affiliation also links it to a national specialist appliance network.

### AI Analysis — not Leon View

`Brand / importer → Sinersis central framework layer → GRUDESA regional platform → Tien21 / associated stores → consumer`
