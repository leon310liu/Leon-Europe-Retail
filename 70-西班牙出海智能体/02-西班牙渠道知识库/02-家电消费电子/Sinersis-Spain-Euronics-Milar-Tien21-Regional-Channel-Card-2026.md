# Sinersis Spain — Euronics / Milar / Tien21 Regional Buying Group Channel Card (2026)

Last verified: 2026-08-30

## 1. PUBLIC FACTS — GROUP IDENTITY AND SCALE

- Sinersis presents itself as a national organization for specialist household-appliance retail in Spain and operates the retail banners **Tien21, Milar and Euronics**.
- Its current homepage states **more than 1,500 stores in Spain**, **more than €1.0bn aggregate turnover**, and more than 30 years of experience.
- The same homepage currently displays banner-level store counts of **746 Tien21, 385 Milar and 380 Euronics**. These sum to 1,511 and should be treated as a current official website snapshot rather than a permanently stable network count.
- Sinersis is a member of Euronics International.

Sources:
- Sinersis official homepage: https://www.sinersis.es/

## 2. PUBLIC FACTS — CENTRAL / REGIONAL PROCUREMENT AND LOGISTICS STRUCTURE

Sinersis' public materials show that its operating model is not simply a single national chain with one centralized warehouse.

- The homepage states that Sinersis coordinates the commercial interests of its regional associated platforms.
- Sinersis says its central organization directs brand marketing strategies and **negotiates framework and promotional agreements with suppliers** (`negociación de los acuerdos marco y promocionales con los proveedores`).
- The official Associates page states that the regional associates manage logistics for **more than 1,500 points of sale** and that **19 warehouses** across Spain coordinate supply and logistics within their areas of influence.
- The Associates page lists the services of regional associates as including **purchasing management, store development, brand-image implementation, shelf/space optimization, promotional campaigns, marketing, logistics optimization and training**.

This is strong public evidence for a federated structure:

**Sinersis national central organization → regional associated platforms / warehouses → local Tien21 / Milar / Euronics points of sale**

What is publicly verified:
- national level: framework/promotional supplier negotiation and brand/marketing strategy;
- regional level: purchasing management, supply/logistics and store support.

What is **NOT publicly verified**:
- who has final category-level approval for a new small domestic appliance supplier;
- whether all regional platforms must list every centrally negotiated SKU;
- whether the central organization, regional platform or individual store places the legal purchase order for each commercial model;
- exact inventory ownership and invoice flow by category/platform.

Sources:
- Sinersis official homepage: https://www.sinersis.es/
- Sinersis official Associates page: https://www.sinersis.es/asociados/

## 3. PUBLIC FACTS — CURRENT CATEGORY SCALE RELEVANT TO 001

Electromarket's **2025-2026 sector report**, reporting 2025 figures, gives the following Sinersis network counts:

- Tien21: **555 points of sale**
- Milar: **366**
- Euronics: **343**

The same report states that Sinersis had the highest business volume among the reported Spanish buying groups in several categories, including **PAE / small domestic appliances: €103.2m**, as well as €487.2m in white goods, €83.2m in brown goods, €45.6m in air conditioning and €55.2m in technology.

This PAE figure is directly relevant to the 001 AI hair-dryer project because hair dryers are a small-domestic-appliance / personal-care appliance product type.

Source:
- Electromarket, Informe del Sector 2025-2026: https://www.electromarket.com/revista/378/45/

## 4. DATA GOVERNANCE — ACTIVE OFFICIAL COUNT CONFLICT

### OPEN DATA CONFLICT / VERIFY BEFORE EXTERNAL USE

Sinersis' own current website is internally inconsistent on the number of regional associates:

- Homepage: **17 regional associated platforms / 17 associates**.
- Official Associates page: **16 associates**.

The Associates page additionally states **19 warehouses**.

Do **not** silently normalize 16 and 17 into one figure. Until Sinersis resolves or clarifies the website, external-facing work should cite the specific page and date of retrieval.

There is also a second scale difference that should not be treated as an error without context:

- Sinersis current homepage snapshot shows 746 Tien21 + 385 Milar + 380 Euronics = **1,511** stores.
- Electromarket's 2025 sector table reports 555 + 366 + 343 = **1,264** points of sale for Sinersis in 2025.

These figures may reflect different reference dates, inclusion rules, network reorganization or database update timing. They should therefore be recorded as separate source/time snapshots rather than merged.

Sources:
- https://www.sinersis.es/
- https://www.sinersis.es/asociados/
- https://www.electromarket.com/revista/378/45/

## 6. 001 AI HAIR DRYER — INFERENCE, NOT PUBLIC FACT

**INFERENCE:** For 001, Sinersis should not be modeled as one undifferentiated national buyer. The public evidence suggests that market-entry research should separately map:

1. **Sinersis central** — framework agreement, group strategy, promotional agreement and possible national assortment program;
2. **regional associated platforms** — purchasing management, supply, warehouse/logistics and execution within their territories;
3. **retail banners / stores** — sell-through and local retail execution.

This does **not** prove that a new Chinese hair-dryer brand must first sign centrally, nor that a regional platform can independently onboard 001. Those entry rules remain to be verified through buyer/supplier evidence.

The 2025 public figure of **€103.2m PAE sales** does, however, establish Sinersis as a material Spanish small-domestic-appliance channel worthy of direct 001 qualification.
