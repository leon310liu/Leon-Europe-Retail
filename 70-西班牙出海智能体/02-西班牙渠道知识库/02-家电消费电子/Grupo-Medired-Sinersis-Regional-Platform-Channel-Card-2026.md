## 1. Entity snapshot

**Entity:** Grupo Medired / MEDIRED  
**Channel role (public fact):** specialist retail-group / regional platform for household appliances, TV and new-technology products.  
**Current group affiliation (public fact):** Sinersis member from **1 January 2026**.  
**Historical affiliation:** public trade-media evidence immediately before the transition still identified Medired with **Grupo Segesa / Cadena Redder** in late 2025. This should be treated as a dated historical state, not as the current 2026 structure.

Sinersis' current official Medired page states that MEDIRED was constituted in September 2008 and operates a network of specialist stores selling household appliances, television and new-technology products.

## 2. 2026 affiliation change — high-priority data-governance fact

### Public facts

- Electromarket reported on **19 December 2025** that Grupo Medired would become a Sinersis member effective **1 January 2026**.
- infoRETAIL reported the same effective date and described the move as part of Sinersis' strategy to strengthen specialist proximity retail.
- Sonitrón reported on **9 January 2026** that Grupo Medired had already joined Sinersis on **1 January 2026**.
- Sinersis now maintains an official associated-platform page for **Medired**, confirming it as a current associated platform.
- By contrast, an Electro-Imagen Comunidad Valenciana roundtable published **31 December 2025** still labelled MEDIRED as **Grupo Segesa-Cadena Redder**. Because that item predates the effective date by one day, it is historically consistent with the migration rather than proof of a 2026 affiliation conflict.

### Knowledge-base rule

For dates **from 2026-01-01 onward**, classify Grupo Medired under **Sinersis** unless a newer authoritative source proves another change.

For pre-2026 records, preserve the historical Segesa / Cadena Redder label when the source is explicitly dated, rather than rewriting history retroactively.

This migration matters because historical trade directories and event participant lists can remain searchable after the underlying group affiliation has changed.

## 4. Channel function and position inside Sinersis

### Public facts

Sinersis' official site describes its operating model as a central organization coordinating regional platforms and negotiating **framework and promotional agreements with suppliers**. It states that the associated regional platforms coordinate procurement and logistics in their areas of influence.

Sinersis' current Medired page says Medired serves its partners, customers and suppliers and operates a specialist retail network.

### Analytical inference — NOT public admission criteria

The most reasonable working model for channel mapping is:

**Supplier / EU importer → Sinersis central framework negotiation → Medired regional platform / associated network → specialist stores → consumer**

This is an analytical representation of the public organizational structure. It must **not** be interpreted as proof that every SKU is approved centrally, ordered by Medired, stocked by Medired, or invoiced through a single legal entity.

## 6. Sinersis network-count inconsistency discovered in this run

Sinersis' current public pages are not internally synchronized:

- the current homepage states that Sinersis coordinates **17 regional platforms**;
- the `Sobre nosotros` page states **16 socios**;
- the homepage also shows more than 1,500 stores and individual banner counts for Tien21, Milar and Euronics.

Because Medired joined on 2026-01-01, the 16-versus-17 discrepancy may reflect page-update timing, but this is **inference only**.

**Knowledge-base rule:** use the current homepage's **17 regional platforms** only with page/date context; do not silently overwrite older 16-platform figures in historical records. Mark aggregate platform counts as date-sensitive.
