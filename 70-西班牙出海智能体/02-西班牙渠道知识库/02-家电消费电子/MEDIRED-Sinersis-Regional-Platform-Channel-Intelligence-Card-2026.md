## 1. Entity / role

**MEDIRED COMERCIAL S.L.** is a Spanish appliance wholesale / buying-management entity based in Paterna (Valencia). Its own website describes MEDIRED as an `Electrodomésticos & Electrónica` chain and a **Central de Gestión de Compras y Ventas**, supporting associated stores in purchasing, commercial-space management, sales and digitalisation.

Its website privacy notice identifies the legal entity as **MEDIRED COMERCIAL S.L.**, CIF **B98080500**, at C/ Botiguers 5, 46980 Paterna (Valencia).

## 2. 2026 structural change: SEGESA → Sinersis

This is the key verified structural change.

- In late-2025 professional-sector coverage, MEDIRED was still identified as **`Grupo Segesa-Cadena Redder`**. Electro-Imagen's Comunidad Valenciana roundtable published on 2025-12-31 labels MEDIRED that way.
- Sinersis announced on 2025-12-19 that **Grupo Medired would join Sinersis effective 2026-01-01**.
- By March 2026, sector coverage already identified **Comercial Benajes – Medired (Sinersis)**.
- Sinersis's own current associated-platform page lists MEDIRED as an associated member, and MEDIRED's own homepage now states that it belongs to **SINERSIS** and **EURONICS INTERNACIONAL**.

Therefore, any database entry still classifying MEDIRED as a current SEGESA / Redder platform is stale for 2026 and should be updated.
