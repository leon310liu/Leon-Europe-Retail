# COMELSA / Milar — East Spain Regional Buying Platform Channel Card (2026)

> Scope: Spain consumer electronics / home appliances channel knowledge base. Focus on public facts relevant to small domestic appliances (PAE) and 001 AI hair dryer. This file does not create or modify Leon View.

## 1. Entity and role

### Public facts
- COMELSA (Comercializadora Electrodomésticos, S.A.) is described by Sinersis as a leading purchasing center / distribution platform for household appliances.
- Its commercial area covers Comunidad Valenciana, Albacete, Teruel, Murcia and Baleares.
- COMELSA operates mainly under the Milar banner.
- Sinersis currently states that COMELSA has more than 90 stores in its network.

### Source
- Sinersis associated-platform page: https://www.sinersis.es/asociado/comelsa/

## 2. Position inside Sinersis

### Public facts
- Sinersis currently describes itself as the organization coordinating the commercial interests of its regional platforms.
- Sinersis states that the regional associates manage purchasing, point-of-sale development, promotional campaigns and logistics.
- Sinersis states that framework and promotional agreements with suppliers are negotiated centrally at Sinersis level, while regional platforms coordinate procurement and logistics in their territories.

### Source
- Sinersis home page: https://www.sinersis.es/
- Sinersis associates page: https://www.sinersis.es/asociados/

## 3. Store network and logistics footprint

### Public facts
- Current Sinersis COMELSA page: 90+ stores.
- A 2022 Sinersis/Alimarket item reported 92 establishments, of which 17 were company-operated and the remainder mostly associated stores.
- The same 2022 source reported that COMELSA expanded its Picanya (Valencia) logistics facilities from 12,000 m² to 15,000 m².

### Data-governance note
- Treat 92 stores / 17 owned stores as a historical 2022 snapshot, not a current 2026 count unless reconfirmed.
- Treat 15,000 m² as a 2022 logistics-facility figure that remains useful for structural context but should be revalidated before external use as a current capacity figure.

### Source
- Sinersis / Alimarket, 8 Feb 2022: https://www.sinersis.es/comelsa-milar-amplia-sus-instalaciones-logisticas-hasta-los-15-000-m2/

## 4. 001 AI hair dryer relevance

### Public facts
- Sinersis/Milar currently sells modern high-speed / digital small-appliance hair dryers through the broader network.
- Example: Milar lists Cecotec DryNova Red Spark 1600W online, showing that hair dryers are actively merchandised in the Milar channel.
- Across the Sinersis network, Tien21 currently lists Ufesa BLDC Ion-Plasma Luxe 1600W, with digital display and brushless-oriented positioning.
- Euronics currently lists Cecotec DryGlam Nano Plasma with 110,000 rpm brushless motor at €59.90 in the captured public listing.

### Important boundary

## 5. Procurement structure — facts vs inference

### Public facts
- Sinersis says it negotiates framework and promotional agreements with suppliers.
- Sinersis says its regional associates perform purchasing management and logistics.
- COMELSA is one of those regional platforms and operates a large regional store network.

### Inference / to verify
A plausible operating model for a new brand is:

Supplier / importer → Sinersis central framework negotiation → COMELSA regional purchasing / logistics execution → Milar stores / associated retailers

This is an inference from the public operating structure, not a published COMELSA new-vendor entry rule.
