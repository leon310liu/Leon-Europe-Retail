## 3. AI Analysis — not Leon View

### 3.1 Technical differentiation pressure for 001

The following feature combination is already present in the Spanish market through SOGO:

**110k+ rpm BLDC + ionic + intelligent/PID temperature control + magnetic accessories + LED display**.

Therefore, 001 cannot rely on “110k rpm / BLDC / ionic / intelligent temperature control” alone as a premium differentiation claim.

Additional differentiation should be tested in areas such as:

- weight and ergonomic balance;
- noise level;
- true temperature-control logic and hair-protection result;
- airflow efficiency rather than rpm alone;
- continuous professional-use durability;
- filter maintenance and motor protection;
- accessory ecosystem;
- repair / SAT and spare-parts system;
- channel margin structure;
- consumer-visible proof of the AI feature.

### 3.2 Competitive-price signal

The captured Conforama price (€44.99) shows that a technically modern BLDC / high-speed / PID-control product can exist at a mass-market retail price well below premium Dyson / Laifen positioning.

This should be treated as a **price-competition signal**, not as a stable market-price benchmark, because retail prices are dynamic and can reflect promotions, lifecycle stage or channel strategy.

### 3.3 Channel implication

SOGO is useful as a benchmark for a **Spain-local brand / importer ecosystem + retailer-operated distribution** model.

The presence of the SEC-SS-3235 at Conforama means future channel research should distinguish:

- local-brand B2B distribution;
- retailer self-operated procurement;
- retailer marketplace listing;
- professional salon distribution;
- DTC / wholesale models.
