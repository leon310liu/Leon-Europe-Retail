# COMELSA / Sinersis Regional Platform Channel Intelligence Card

> Last verified: 2026-08-28

> Leon View: **None added in this file.**

## 1. Entity / channel role

**Public facts**

- COMELSA (Comercializadora Electrodomésticos, S.A.) describes itself as a purchasing and services central / distributor for the **Milar** channel in Spain's Levante area and forms part of **Sinersis**.
- COMELSA states that it operates in **Comunidad Valenciana, Murcia, Teruel, Albacete and Islas Baleares**.
- Sinersis currently describes COMELSA as a leading purchasing central in appliance distribution with **90+ stores**.
- COMELSA's own current website gives a more specific current network figure of **92 points of sale**.

**Sources**

- Sinersis — COMELSA: https://www.sinersis.es/asociado/comelsa/
- COMELSA — Home: https://www.comelsa.es/
- COMELSA — Store locator: https://www.comelsa.es/encuentra-tu-tienda-milar-mas-cercana/

## 2. Procurement and operating structure

**Public facts**

COMELSA's current `Áreas de trabajo` page states that its commercial/marketing function includes:

- **direct negotiation with principal suppliers** on offers, campaigns and protocols;
- real-time availability of **13,000+ references** and **230 brands**;
- a proprietary point-of-sale management environment / intranet connecting the central and stores, covering orders, stock, prices and invoices.

This provides direct evidence that COMELSA is not only a logistics node: it has supplier-negotiation, assortment-information and store-management functions at regional-platform level.

**Source**

- COMELSA — Áreas de trabajo: https://www.comelsa.es/areas-de-trabajo-en-las-tiendas-milar/

## 3. Network model

**Public facts**

- COMELSA's history page says the company grew from an association of appliance retailers and today includes both shareholder and non-shareholder members.
- The company describes itself as the **Milar central distributor for the Levante zone**.
- Its network consists of local/proximity Milar stores rather than a pure wholly-owned chain.
- Historical Sinersis reporting (2022) stated that COMELSA managed 92 stores, of which **17 were company-operated** and the remainder were associated stores; this ownership split is historical and should **not** be assumed unchanged in 2026 without fresh confirmation.

**Sources**

- COMELSA — Historia: https://www.comelsa.es/historia-comelsa/
- Sinersis — 2022 logistics expansion note: https://www.sinersis.es/comelsa-milar-amplia-sus-instalaciones-logisticas-hasta-los-15-000-m2/

## 4. Logistics baseline

**Public facts**

- Sinersis reported in 2022 that COMELSA expanded its Picanya logistics facilities to **15,000 m²**.
- COMELSA's current store-opening page advertises **24-hour deliveries** as part of its support proposition to Milar retailers.

**Data boundary**

- The **15,000 m²** figure is a 2022 public baseline and has not been re-verified as a changed/current 2026 warehouse-area figure.
- “24-hour deliveries” is a current marketing statement; precise geographic/SLA conditions are not publicly specified and require commercial verification.

**Sources**

- https://www.sinersis.es/comelsa-milar-amplia-sus-instalaciones-logisticas-hasta-los-15-000-m2/
- https://www.comelsa.es/abre-tu-tienda-de-electrodomesticos-milar/

## 5. Supplier-access evidence

**Public facts**

- COMELSA has historically organised member conventions where major suppliers present innovations directly to COMELSA members. The 2018 convention publicly listed Samsung, Hisense, LG, Whirlpool and Santander Consumer among participating partners.
- This is historical evidence of a **regional platform → supplier presentation → member-store** interaction mechanism.

**Important boundary**

The public convention evidence is historical. It proves that such a mechanism has existed, but does **not** prove that the same event format, supplier roster or buying authority remains unchanged in 2026.

**Source**

- COMELSA — III Convención Comelsa-Milar: https://www.comelsa.es/noticias/convecion-comelsa-milar/

## 7. AI Analysis — 001 AI Hair Dryer relevance

**Inference, not public fact**

COMELSA should be treated as a **high-relevance Sinersis regional-platform candidate** for 001 AI Hair Dryer because:

1. it explicitly performs regional supplier negotiation;
2. it operates a 92-point Milar network in a commercially important Levante / Mediterranean region;
3. it handles PAE campaigns;
4. it has centralised systems for orders, stocks, prices and invoices.

A plausible entry hypothesis is:

**Brand / Spain importer → COMELSA regional platform → regional commercial conditions / assortment → Milar stores → consumer**

However, public evidence is **not sufficient** to conclude that COMELSA alone has final SKU-selection authority for hair dryers, that it can onboard a new brand independently of Sinersis central agreements, or that a regional pilot can automatically scale to the full Sinersis network.
