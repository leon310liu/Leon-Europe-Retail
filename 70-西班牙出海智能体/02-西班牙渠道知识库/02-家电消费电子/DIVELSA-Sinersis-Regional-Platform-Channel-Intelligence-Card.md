## 3. Data Reconciliation / Known Conflicts

### 3.1 150+ points of sale vs 120 associated stores

The following figures must **not** be treated as the same metric:

- Sinersis current page: **150+ points of sale**, including owned and associated stores.
- 2025 annual convention: **120 associated Euronics/Tien21 stores** participating in the event.

Possible explanations include different network scope, active attendees vs total network, owned vs associated stores, and geography/banner coverage. There is not enough public evidence to collapse these values into one number.

**Knowledge rule:** store counts must preserve metric type, source page and verification date.

### 3.2 Historical 183-store figure is obsolete for current sizing

Older 2019 public material referenced **183 associated stores**. That number should remain historical only and must not be used as a 2026 current network figure.

## 4. Procurement / Supplier Structure — Public Evidence

The 2025 commercial convention provides direct evidence that DIVELSA acts as more than a logistics warehouse:

- it convenes associated stores;
- it brings major brands and suppliers into a regional commercial fair;
- PAE suppliers are explicitly included;
- suppliers present product ranges/new products and commercial offers to the network.

This supports a regional supplier-entry mechanism of:

**Brand / supplier → DIVELSA regional organization → commercial fair / product presentation / commercial conditions → associated Euronics & Tien21 stores**

However, public sources do **not** prove that DIVELSA has final autonomous buying authority for every SKU, nor that a regional acceptance automatically creates a Sinersis-wide listing.

## 5. AI Analysis — not Leon View

For 001 AI Hair Dryer, DIVELSA is a meaningful regional validation target because:

1. PAE brands already participate in its supplier conventions.
2. It combines wholesale/distribution infrastructure with a proximity-retail network.
3. The network spans a commercially important eastern/southeastern Spain footprint.
4. A regional test could be operationally more realistic than assuming immediate Sinersis-wide rollout.

But the following must remain unverified until commercial contact:
