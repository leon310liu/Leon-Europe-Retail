## 3. Buying structure — unusually important for supplier-entry research

### FACT — Medired describes itself as a buying-and-sales management centre
Medired official homepage currently describes the organisation as a **“Central de Gestión de Compras y Ventas”**, supporting associates in purchasing, commercial-space management, sales and digitalisation.

Source: https://medired.eu/

### FACT — supplier negotiations are coordinated centrally
In a 2025 interview, Medired manager Gerardo Montalvá stated that Medired provides its members with purchasing support and **continuously negotiates conditions with the sector's principal suppliers**.

Source: https://www.electro-imagen.com/articulo/en-medired-priorizamos-el-crecimiento-de-nuestros-asociados-mas-que-nuestra-propia-expansion-territorial

### FACT — no conventional central warehouse
The same interview gives an unusually clear description of Medired's logistics model:

- Medired says it **does not operate its own warehouse**;
- stock is held in the warehouses of member companies;
- those member warehouses are connected to the central organisation;
- the central organisation can monitor product status and rotation;
- the network reported **40,000+ units in stock** at the time of the interview.

This is materially different from a classic regional buying platform with one large central warehouse.

Source: https://www.electro-imagen.com/articulo/en-medired-priorizamos-el-crecimiento-de-nuestros-asociados-mas-que-nuestra-propia-expansion-territorial

## 6. 001 AI hair dryer relevance

### FACT

Source: https://www.medired-nntt.com/articulos/rem-pae-sec-ionic-dry-2200

### NOT YET VERIFIED
No reliable current public evidence found in this research pass that Medired itself / its member network currently lists a **100,000–120,000 rpm BLDC high-speed hair dryer** comparable to 001.

Therefore do **not** write “Medired already sells 001-type high-speed dryers” as fact.

### INFERENCE — separate from public fact
Because Medired is a PAE-active buying/sales management platform and publicly describes central supplier-condition negotiation while member warehouses hold stock, it is reasonable to test Medired as a **regional supplier-entry node** for 001 rather than approaching dozens of stores independently.

This is an inference, not a published supplier-entry rule.

## 8. Relationship with Sinersis master model

Sinersis currently states that it negotiates framework and promotional agreements with suppliers while its regional associates provide purchasing management and logistics services.

Medired adds an important variation to this model because its own public description says **the regional platform has no conventional central warehouse and relies on connected member warehouses**.

Therefore the Spain channel AI should not assume that all Sinersis regional platforms share one identical logistics architecture.
