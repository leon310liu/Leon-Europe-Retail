# Spain PAE Operator Baseline 2025–2026

## Scope
Public-fact baseline for Spain's small domestic appliance (PAE) operator landscape, intended for consumer-electronics market-entry research and 001 AI Hair Dryer benchmarking.

## Public facts
Source: Electromarket, Electroguía / Informe del Sector 2025–2026.

Selected companies reported under PAE-related activity in Spain (2025 estimate / latest table values, EUR millions):

- De'Longhi Electrodomésticos España, S.L.: ~€110m
- Electrodomésticos Taurus, S.L.: ~€78m
- B&B Trends, S.L.: ~€55m
- Electrodomésticos Jata, S.A.: ~€27m
- River International, S.A.: ~€27m
- Spectrum Brands, S.L.U.: ~€19m
- Varta Remington Rayovac Spain, S.L.: ~€8m

The same source shows that PAE remains a large and competitive segment in Spain and that operator scale varies substantially across brand owners, local manufacturers and distributors.

## Data boundary
These figures are company-level / operator-level turnover figures reported in an industry table. They are NOT:

- hair-dryer category sales,
- personal-care-only turnover,
- brand-only turnover where a company operates several brands,
- audited market-share figures for hair dryers.

Do not use these values to infer hair-dryer market share.

## AI analysis (not Leon View)
For 001 AI Hair Dryer, the competitive set should not be reduced to retail shelf brands alone. It also includes sizeable Spain-based PAE operators and distributors that already control brand portfolios, channel relationships, logistics and after-sales capabilities.

This suggests a separate diligence layer for potential partners/competitors:

1. Operator legal entity
2. Brand portfolio
3. Personal Care / Hair Care exposure
4. Importer-of-record role
5. RII-AEE / EPR responsibility
6. National distribution coverage
7. Existing retail / Buying Group relationships
8. SAT / spare-parts capability
9. Own-brand / Private Label conflict
