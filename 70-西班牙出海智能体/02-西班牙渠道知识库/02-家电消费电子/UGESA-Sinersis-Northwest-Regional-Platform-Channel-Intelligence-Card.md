> Last verified: 2026-08-28

## 1. Entity / channel role

**Public facts**

- **Unión Gallega de Electrodomésticos S.A. (UGESA)** describes itself as a reference **purchasing central (central de compras)** for appliances and consumer electronics in northwest Spain, operating since 1982.
- UGESA currently states that it has **38+ socios** and **150+ asociados**, representing the **Tien21** and **Euronics** banners in **Galicia, Asturias and León**.
- UGESA forms part of **Sinersis**.
- Sinersis independently describes UGESA as the reference appliance / consumer-electronics purchasing central for the northwest and repeats the current 38+ socios / 150+ asociados scope.

**Sources**

- UGESA — Sobre nosotros: https://www.ugesa.es/nosotros
- Sinersis — UGESA: https://www.sinersis.es/asociado/ugesa/

## 2. Procurement structure

**Public facts**

- The company explicitly uses the term **central de compras**, which is stronger evidence of a purchasing role than a generic logistics-platform description.
- Sinersis states that UGESA's purpose is to provide associated retailers with improved economic conditions and business tools.
- UGESA's current homepage says its assortment contains **12,000+ references** and that it works with leading brands.
- Historical UGESA reporting (2016) described more than 12,000 references physically available in its warehouse and a maximum 24-hour delivery promise. This historical statement supports the existence of central inventory/logistics capability, but the precise 24-hour SLA must not be treated as a current 2026 fact without fresh confirmation.

**Sources**

- UGESA — Home: https://www.ugesa.es/
- UGESA — 2016 expansion / services note: https://www.ugesa.es/noticias/ugesa-continua-su-expansion
- Sinersis — UGESA: https://www.sinersis.es/asociado/ugesa/

## 3. Geographic and retail-network model

**Public facts**

- UGESA's current defined operating area is **Galicia, Asturias and León**.
- The regional network operates under **Tien21** and **Euronics** banners.
- UGESA's own banner page markets these banners to independent/local appliance retailers and offers prospective stores information about joining the network.

**Important boundary**

- National Tien21 / Euronics store counts shown on UGESA's banner page are **national brand-network figures**, not UGESA regional network counts.
- `38+ socios`, `150+ asociados`, and national banner store counts are different variables and must not be merged into one “store count”.

**Sources**

- UGESA — Enseñas: https://www.ugesa.es/ensenas
- UGESA — Sobre nosotros: https://www.ugesa.es/nosotros

## 4. Regional marketing and commercial execution

**Public facts**

UGESA currently runs separate regional promotional calendars for both banners:

- **Tien21** campaigns are planned to drive final consumers to member points of sale through online and offline promotions.
- **Euronics** campaigns are also centrally planned by UGESA to give visibility to participating stores.
- Current 2026 examples include Tien21 `TOP21 Mayo 2026`, `Folleto Verano 2026`, and Euronics `Folleto Verano 2026`.

This is direct evidence that UGESA performs regional commercial/marketing execution in addition to purchasing-central functions.

## 5. PAE relevance

**Public facts**

- UGESA has run dedicated **PAE (Pequeño Aparato Electrodoméstico)** campaigns in its regional Tien21 network.
- A current archive entry shows an **Especial PAE** campaign running from 1–30 September 2025.
- The May 2026 TOP21 campaign explicitly includes **pequeño electrodoméstico** among promoted categories.

This confirms that UGESA's regional commercial scope includes **small domestic appliances**, not only major appliances / white goods.

## 6. Association / RAEE ecosystem signal

**Public facts**

- Sinersis states that UGESA belongs to **ADEN (Asociación Nacional de Empresas Distribuidoras de Electrodomésticos)**.
- Sinersis/UGESA describe ADEN as supporting small appliance retailers and contributing to compliance with electrical/electronic waste rules.

**Boundary**

Membership in ADEN does **not** prove that UGESA will assume a new supplier's Importer of Record, RII-AEE/EPR or RAEE legal obligations. Those responsibilities require contract-level verification.

**Source**

- https://www.sinersis.es/asociado/ugesa/

## 7. AI Analysis — 001 AI Hair Dryer relevance

**Inference, not public fact**

UGESA should be treated as a **medium-to-high relevance northwest regional-platform candidate** for 001 AI Hair Dryer because:

1. it is explicitly a regional **purchasing central**;
2. it covers Galicia, Asturias and León through 150+ associated points / businesses under Tien21 and Euronics;
3. it actively commercialises PAE and executes regional promotional campaigns;
4. it maintains a large multi-brand assortment and centralised warehouse/logistics infrastructure.

A plausible route to test is:

**Brand / Spain importer → UGESA purchasing central → regional assortment / commercial conditions → Tien21 / Euronics associated stores → consumer**

However, public evidence is **not sufficient** to conclude that UGESA has unilateral final SKU authority for hair dryers, that it can onboard a new brand without Sinersis-level framework negotiation, or that a successful northwest listing would automatically expand to other Sinersis platforms.
