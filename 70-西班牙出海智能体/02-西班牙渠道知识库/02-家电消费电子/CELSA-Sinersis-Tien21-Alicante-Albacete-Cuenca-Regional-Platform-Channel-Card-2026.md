## 2. Verified public facts

### 2.1 Current Sinersis regional-platform status

Sinersis currently lists CELSA among its regional associates. Sinersis describes its regional associates collectively as platforms that manage purchasing, point-of-sale development, brand implementation, promotional campaigns, logistics optimisation and training; it also states that the regional-platform network coordinates procurement and logistics for more than 1,500 points of sale.

**Source:** Sinersis, “Asociados” (current page, accessed 2026-08-31)
https://www.sinersis.es/asociados/

### 2.2 CELSA membership / territory

Sinersis' current CELSA profile states that CELSA serves **57 socios** located in **Alicante, Albacete and southern Cuenca**. The same official profile says CELSA provides stores with management software, advertising, **integral logistics**, touchscreen tools, and management of returns and incidents.

**Source:** Sinersis, “Celsa” associate profile (current page, accessed 2026-08-31)
https://www.sinersis.es/asociado/celsa/

### 2.3 2026 operating evidence

Electromarket reported on **10 March 2026** that CELSA-TIEN21 held its annual convention in Alicante on 21–22 February 2026, with participation from practically all associated stores. CELSA presented its **2026 Action Plan**, covering commercial strategy, marketing, advertising and store training. This is strong evidence that CELSA was actively operating as a Tien21 regional platform in 2026, rather than being only a legacy entity in older directories.

**Source:** Electromarket, 10 Mar 2026
https://www.electromarket.com/noticia/33143/celsa-tien21-reune-a-sus-tiendas-asociadas-en-su-convencion-anual.html

**Corroborating source:** Electro-Imagen, 10 Mar 2026
