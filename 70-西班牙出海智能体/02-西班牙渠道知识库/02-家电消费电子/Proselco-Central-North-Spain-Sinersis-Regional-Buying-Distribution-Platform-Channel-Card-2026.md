## 1. Entity snapshot

**PROSELCO, S.A.** is a regional platform within **Sinersis** focused on proximity retail for household appliances and consumer electronics. Its own current website identifies **Tien21** and **Milar** as the retail banners it develops and states that it serves **246 points of sale**.

## 3. Purchasing structure

Proselco publicly describes a dedicated **Purchasing Department** managing **146 brands and 15,000 high-rotation references**. It states that this department works to obtain better commercial conditions, services and promotional actions for associated stores.

This is important because it provides direct public evidence that Proselco is not merely a banner-management entity: it has an active regional purchasing/commercial coordination function.

### Confirmed public structure

**Sinersis national central services**  
→ framework / promotional negotiation and national coordination  
→ **Proselco regional platform**  
→ regional purchasing, logistics, commercial support, marketing and store management tools  
→ Tien21 / Milar associated stores  
→ end customer

Sinersis currently describes itself as coordinating the commercial interests of **17 regional platforms**, including negotiation of framework and promotional agreements with suppliers.

Source: https://www.sinersis.es/

### To Verify

Public sources reviewed do **not** establish:

- which product categories are negotiated centrally by Sinersis versus directly by Proselco;
- who holds final SKU listing authority for PAE / personal-care appliances;
- whether a new supplier can enter only one Proselco territory without a Sinersis-wide agreement;
- regional trial-store procedures;
- MOQ, rebates, payment terms, listing fees, marketing contribution or returns conditions;
- importer-of-record requirements for non-EU suppliers;
- exact allocation of RAEE/WEEE, warranty and SAT responsibilities.

## 4. 2025 Caslesa integration — material structural change

A major change occurred on **1 July 2025**.

Industry media reported that **Caslesa integrated its procurement and commercial-strategy operations into Proselco**. Caslesa continued managing local commercial operations in its territory, while **procurement and commercial strategy were to be carried out jointly from Proselco**.

Sonitrón described the combined structure as the largest associated-distribution group in the northern half of Spain, with **more than 300 stores** identified under the **Milar, Euronics and Tien21** banners and more than **20% share of commercial surface** in that area.

Alimarket independently reported that Caslesa had integrated its **aprovisionamiento** and **estrategia comercial** operations into Proselco from 1 July 2025.

Sources:
- Sonitrón, 15 Jul 2025: https://sonitron.net/caslesa-proselco/
- Alimarket, 14 Jul 2025: https://www.alimarket.es/electro/noticia/411448/la-plataforma-caslesa-se-integra-en-proselco

### Interpretation boundary

**Inference:** this integration likely increases the purchasing volume and supplier-negotiation relevance of Proselco in northern Spain.

**Not established as public fact:** the exact legal merger structure, SKU-by-SKU purchasing authority, consolidated supplier contract ownership, or whether the “300+ stores” figure should replace Proselco's own current “246 points of sale” figure. These figures refer to different scopes and should not be merged without verification.

## 5. 001 AI hair dryer relevance

The 001 product is a consumer-level AI / high-speed hair dryer, which falls within PAE / personal-care electrical appliances.

### Confirmed channel facts relevant to 001

1. Proselco operates household-appliance / consumer-electronics proximity retail under Tien21 and Milar.
2. Proselco has a dedicated purchasing department managing 146 brands and 15,000 references.
3. Proselco has central warehousing and regional delivery infrastructure.
4. Tien21 currently sells **Taurus DIGITAL FORCE AI** hair dryers, confirming that an AI-positioned hair-dryer SKU already exists in the broader Tien21 commercial ecosystem.
5. After the 2025 Caslesa integration, Proselco is involved in a wider north-Spain procurement/commercial-strategy structure.

Source for current Tien21 AI dryer example:
- https://www.tien21.es/taurus-digital-force-ai.html

### Boundary

The Tien21 product listing **does not prove** that Proselco itself buys, stocks or distributes Taurus DIGITAL FORCE AI, nor that all Proselco-linked stores carry it.

### To Verify for 001

- Does Proselco buy personal-care PAE directly or via Sinersis national framework agreements?
- Is there a category buyer specifically responsible for secadores / cuidado personal / PAE?
- Can a new Chinese brand supply Proselco directly?
- Is local Spanish/EU stock mandatory?
- Is a Spanish importer/distributor required?
- Can 001 start through a regional pilot instead of a national Tien21/Milar rollout?
- Required technical documentation beyond CE, EMC/LVD/RoHS and WEEE/RAEE obligations.
- Warranty / SAT service-level expectations.
- Commercial terms: MOQ, payment period, rebate, promotional budget, returns and dead-stock treatment.

## 6. Current-data conflicts / stale-data governance

### Conflict A — Proselco store scale

Public sources now contain several different figures:

- **246 points of sale** — current Proselco homepage.
- **225+ points of sale** — older corporate descriptions still reproduced by third-party databases.
- **250 points of sale** — 2021 reporting after UNIDO joined Proselco.
- **300+ stores** — 2025 Sonitrón description of the combined Proselco + Caslesa structure.

**Knowledge-base rule:** use **246** only when referring to Proselco's own current stated network; use **300+** only when describing the post-July-2025 combined Proselco/Caslesa commercial structure. Do not treat them as directly contradictory without matching scope.

### Conflict B — banners

Proselco's current own “Nuestras enseñas” page prominently lists **Tien21 and Milar**. Older descriptions and 2025 coverage of the combined Proselco/Caslesa structure also reference **Euronics**.

**Status:** `OPEN SCOPE DIFFERENCE / TO VERIFY`.

Do not state that Proselco's current standalone network is uniformly Tien21 + Milar + Euronics unless verified at store/platform level.

### Conflict C — Sinersis scale figures on associate pages

The Proselco page within Sinersis still contains older wording referring to **2,500+ Spanish points of sale** and **13,000+ European points**. Sinersis' current homepage instead describes **17 regional platforms** and Euronics International as **8,800+ stores in 35 countries**.

**Knowledge-base rule:** treat the older 2,500 / 13,000 figures as legacy corporate copy, not current 2026 Sinersis scale.

Sources:
- https://www.sinersis.es/asociado/proselco/
- https://www.sinersis.es/
