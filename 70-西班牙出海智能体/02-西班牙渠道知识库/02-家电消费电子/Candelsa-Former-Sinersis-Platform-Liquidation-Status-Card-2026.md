# Candelsa — Former Sinersis Regional Platform / Liquidation Status Card (2026)

> Knowledge type: Public-fact governance card
> Geography: Cataluña / Baleares / Aragón / Soria / La Rioja / Navarra (historical operating area stated by Candelsa)
> Channel: Home appliances / consumer electronics / regional buying centre / wholesale distribution
> Status date: 2026-08-31
> Leon View: **NONE ADDED**

## 1. Executive status

**Do not model Electrodomèstics Candelsa, S.A. as a current Sinersis regional platform in 2026.**

Public records show a material status change:

1. Sinersis announced on 9 June 2025 the definitive and irrevocable termination of all collaboration contracts with Electrodomèstics Candelsa, S.A., including brand licences, commercial assignments and corporate agreements.
2. Candelsa later entered voluntary insolvency proceedings.
3. The Spanish Mercantile Registry bulletin (BORME) records that a final court order dated 27 November 2025 opened the **liquidation phase** of Electrodomèstics Candelsa, S.A.; the liquidation/disolution entry was registered on 27 January 2026 and published on 4 February 2026.
4. Candelsa's former subsidiary Investing Partnership S.L. (owner of Miró Electrodomèstics) also entered liquidation under a separate proceeding in December 2025 / January 2026.

Therefore, any older dataset that still maps Candelsa to Sinersis/Tien21/Euronics as an active regional purchasing platform must be treated as **STALE / INVALID FOR CURRENT CHANNEL MAPPING**.

## 2. Public facts

### 2.1 Historical operating model

Candelsa's own corporate website describes Electrodomèstics Candelsa, S.A. as a purchasing centre for appliances, consumer electronics and new technologies, founded in 1972.

The site states that it historically served retailers across multiple Spanish autonomous communities and provinces and that its model included:

- central purchasing;
- supply to stores;
- direct delivery to final consumers on behalf of stores;
- logistics infrastructure;
- commercial negotiation based on aggregated purchase volume.

Historical operating area currently displayed on the Candelsa website:

- Cataluña
- Baleares
- Aragón
- Soria
- La Rioja
- Navarra

**Important:** the website remains accessible, but this does **not** establish that the company is operating normally in 2026. Corporate/legal status must take precedence over static website marketing pages.

### 2.2 Break with Sinersis

On 9 June 2025, Sinersis publicly announced the definitive and irrevocable termination of all collaboration agreements with Electrodomèstics Candelsa, S.A.

The announcement explicitly covered:

- licence rights to use Sinersis retail brands;
- commercial assignments / agreements;
- corporate agreements.

This supersedes earlier channel maps that treated Candelsa as a Sinersis regional member/platform.

### 2.3 Insolvency and liquidation

Official BORME record:

- Company: **ELECTRODOMESTICS CANDELSA SA**
- Court: Juzgado de lo Mercantil nº 7 de Barcelona
- Proceeding: Concurso Voluntario 949/2025
- Final court order date: **27 November 2025**
- Event: **Apertura de la fase de liquidación de la sociedad**
- Dissolution: Judicial
- Registry date: **27 January 2026**
- Publication: BORME nº 23, **4 February 2026**

A later BORME entry published 12 February 2026 records closure of the qualification section of the insolvency proceeding. This does not reverse the liquidation opening recorded previously.

### 2.4 Miró / Investing Partnership

Candelsa had announced the acquisition of 100% of Investing Partnership S.L., owner of Miró Electrodomèstics, in March 2025.

Official BORME later records:

- Company: **INVESTING PARTNERSHIP S.L.**
- Proceeding: Concurso Voluntario 1153/2025
- Final court order: **5 December 2025**
- Event: opening of liquidation phase
- Dissolution: Judicial
- Registry publication: January 2026

This is relevant because older public material can still present Miró/Candelsa as an expanding retail-distribution combination, which is no longer a safe current-state assumption.
