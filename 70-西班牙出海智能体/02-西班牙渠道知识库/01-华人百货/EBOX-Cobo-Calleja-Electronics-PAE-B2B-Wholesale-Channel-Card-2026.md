## 1. Channel identity

**Public facts**
- The current website identifies the operator as **Electronica De Consumo Ebox S.L.** and positions it as a **wholesale distributor in Cobo Calleja**.
- It states that it works **exclusively with professionals** and sells to **shops, distributors and e-commerce businesses**.
- Current address published on the website: **C/ Fabero Nº2 Local 14, 28947 Fuenlabrada, Madrid**.
- Current commercial contact channels include WhatsApp and **WeChat**, which is explicitly shown on the website.

Sources:
- https://electronicaebox.com/
- https://electronicaebox.com/contact-us/

## 2. Product scope and business model

**Public facts**
- EBOX states that it specializes in **consumer-electronics accessories and small domestic appliances (pequeño electrodoméstico / PAE)**.
- The website states that it sells to stores, distributors and e-commerce operators across the Peninsula.
- Current site messaging states **minimum order: €200 + VAT** and free shipping above the same published threshold.
- The site also states **“fabricación y diseño propio”** for accessories and small domestic appliances.
- Brands currently displayed include **Ebox, XO, Philips and Muse**, among others.
- The live catalogue includes PAE such as an **Oryx hand blender EEL1266D, 500W**, demonstrating that the PAE positioning is reflected in actual product listings rather than only corporate copy.

Sources:
- https://electronicaebox.com/
- https://electronicaebox.com/product/batidora-de-mano-oryx-eel1266d/

## 3. Evidence of distributor onboarding capability

**Public facts**
- EBOX operates a dedicated **“Flotas y distribuidores”** page for its connected V16 emergency-light product.
- That page explicitly invites professional companies to request a quote and includes a selectable reason **“Ser distribuidor oficial”**.
- The same product is offered in quantity tiers, demonstrating visible wholesale / volume-pricing logic in at least one active category.

Source:
- https://electronicaebox.com/flotas-y-distribuidores/

**Data-governance note**
This proves that EBOX can operate distributor recruitment / volume-sale programs for at least some products. It does **not** prove that the same onboarding terms apply to third-party PAE brands or hair dryers.

These multiple specialist wholesalers show that Cobo Calleja should be modeled not only as a general bazaar sourcing hub but also as a distinct **electronics / PAE wholesale layer** capable of supplying independent retail formats. Public sources do not establish the ethnic ownership or customer nationality of every operator, so this must not be simplified into “Chinese-owned channel = Chinese consumer”.
