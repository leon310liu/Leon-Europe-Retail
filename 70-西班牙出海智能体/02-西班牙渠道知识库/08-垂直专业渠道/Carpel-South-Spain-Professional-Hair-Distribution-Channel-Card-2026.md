## 2. Professional customer structure / B2B mechanics

### Public facts
Carpel operates a protected professional channel rather than only a consumer webshop.

The official site currently states:
- verified professionals can access **wholesale prices across the store**;
- professional verification can be completed by uploading **autónomo registration or CIF**;
- verification is generally completed in **24–48 hours**;
- the basic professional account has no subscription fee and its verification is valid for **12 months**;
- the optional **Carpel PRO** plan is advertised at **€6.90/month** and adds benefits including quarterly rebates of up to **5%**, double points and lower free-shipping thresholds;
- the company also states that professional conditions are intended for **peluquerías, barberías and distribuidores** with validated B2B status.

Important boundary:
These are **downstream customer conditions**. They must NOT be treated as Carpel's supplier onboarding terms, supplier rebates, supplier payment terms or supplier MOQ.

## 6. Data-quality conflicts / governance notes

### Conflict A — store count
The official site repeatedly states **5 physical stores**, but the current store page explicitly lists only **4 store locations**: Huelva, Lepe, Ayamonte and Ávila.

Knowledge-base handling:
- retain **5 physical stores** as the company's current stated headline figure;
- retain the four named locations as verified listed locations;
- mark the identity/location of the fifth store as **To Verify**;
- do not invent the missing fifth location.

### Conflict B — business age
The site currently contains several inconsistent age references:
- "Más de 40 años" / "40 años" on the stores page;
- "Desde 1997" and "1997 Año de constitución como S.L." on current company pages;
- another footer element says "Desde 1997 +25 años en el sector".

Knowledge-base handling:
- treat **1997 as the stated incorporation year of the S.L.**;
- treat "40+ years" as a broader claimed business/trade-history statement;
- do not equate the two until independent corporate-history evidence is obtained.
