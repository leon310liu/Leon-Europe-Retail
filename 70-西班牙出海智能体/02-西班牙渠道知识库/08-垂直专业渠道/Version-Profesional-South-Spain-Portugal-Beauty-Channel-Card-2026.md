## 1. Channel identity

### Public facts
- Versión Profesional states that it was founded in 1993 and operates in professional beauty / hairdressing distribution.
- Its public company profile describes five commercial formats, **50+ stores mainly across southern Spain and Portugal**, a sales-representative network, a **professional-only Cash & Carry**, telesales and e-commerce.
- The company sells both wholesale and retail, serving professional customers as well as the general public.
- Its current official e-commerce site states that it carries **30,000+ references** in hairdressing and beauty products.

## 3. Hair-tool and dryer category evidence

### Public facts
Versión Profesional currently has a dedicated `Secadores de mano` category and lists multiple professional hair dryers.

Directly relevant current examples include:
- **Multy Dry**: brushless motor, **110,000 rpm**, ionic technology, 6 interchangeable accessories, 3 speeds, 3 temperature levels and memory function.
- **Eolux EX01**: compact professional bladeless ionic dryer with brushless motor and intelligent temperature control (currently shown out of stock at verification time).
- **ghd Helios Black / White**: professional ionic high-speed dryers.
