# Fama Fabré × Parlux Spain — Professional Hair Distributor Channel Card (2026)

> Research scope: public facts only. Inference is separated. No Leon View is created in this file.

## 1. Channel identity

- Company: **FAMA FABRÉ S.A.U.**
- Location: L'Hospitalet de Llobregat, Barcelona, Spain.
- Public positioning: wholesale distributor for **hairdressing, barbering, aesthetics and makeup**.
- Public company profile states the business was founded in **1946** and operates as a wholesale company in the professional beauty sector.
- Source: https://es.linkedin.com/company/famafabre

## 2. Parlux distribution role in Spain

### Public fact

Parlux's own official website has repeatedly identified **Fama Fabré as its distributor for Spain**. Parlux's 2019 Salon Look report states that the Parlux stand in Madrid was organised by its distributor for Spain, Fama Fabré.

- Parlux official source: https://www.parlux.it/blogs/news/parlux-alyon%C2%AE-parlux-ardent%C2%AE-e-parlux-3200%C2%AE-plus

A Fama Fabré-hosted Parlux catalogue labels **FAMA FABRÉ S.A.U.** as **“Distribuidor Mayorista Oficial”** for Spain and provides the Barcelona address and contact details.

- Catalogue source: https://www.famafabre.com/img/cms/FamaFabre/Catalogos/PARLUX%202024_ES%20ENG.pdf

### Freshness verification

BeautyMarket reported on **8 May 2025** that Fama Fabré launched the new **Parlux Elysium** in Spain and described Fama Fabré as the **exclusive distributor in the Spanish market** for this launch.

- Source: https://www.beautymarket.es/peluqueria/fama-fabre-lanza-en-espana-el-nuevo-parlux-elysium-innovacion-digital-para-el-secado-profesional-peluqueria-35583.php

## 3. 001 AI hair-dryer relevance

Parlux is a professional hair-dryer specialist brand with strong salon positioning. The 2025 Elysium launch provides a recent example of a professional dryer entering Spain through a specialised national distributor rather than directly through a mass retailer.

The Elysium is described as:
- professional digital motor;
- 2300 W;
- 80 m³/h airflow;
- digital interface and memory function;
- professional salon-use positioning.

This makes Fama Fabré a relevant benchmark for studying **professional hair-tool importer/distributor routes** for 001.

## 4. Publicly evidenced route

**Parlux / manufacturer → Fama Fabré / Spanish professional distributor → salon / barber / professional beauty channel**

The first two links are publicly evidenced. The exact downstream account structure, dealer tiers and regional reseller network are not fully public in the sources reviewed.

## 5. Inference — not public fact

For 001, a specialised professional distributor similar to Fama Fabré could represent an alternative route to entering Spain compared with direct listing at mass beauty retailers or consumer-electronics chains.

This is an **inference only**. It does not establish that Fama Fabré accepts new Chinese brands or that it is a suitable target account for 001.
