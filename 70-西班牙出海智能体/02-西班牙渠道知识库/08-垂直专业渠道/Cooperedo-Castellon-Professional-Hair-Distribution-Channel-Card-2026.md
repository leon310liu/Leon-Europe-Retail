## 1. Channel identity

### Public facts
- Cooperedo is a Castellón-based family business focused on the distribution and sale of professional hairdressing and beauty products.
- Its official website states that the business began in the 1940s and that Cooperedo S.L. was “created officially” in 1999.
- The current operating footprint described by Cooperedo is provincial rather than national: distribution and sales to professionals and consumers across the province of Castellón.
- The company currently describes **two stores open to the public plus a third location exclusively for professionals / members**.
- Its official current address for member sales is C/ Onda 15, Castellón de la Plana; public/professional retail is listed at C/ San Vicente 60.

## 2. Commercial structure and customer model

### Public facts
Cooperedo publicly describes a mixed structure:
1. distribution to professional salons across Castellón province;
2. two stores serving both professionals and the general public;
3. one professional/member-only sales point;
4. planned delivery routes for professional customers.

Its current commercial policy explicitly states:
- **maximum discount from the first unit**;
- **no minimum quantity imposed on professional customers**;
- no forced stock accumulation;
- planned delivery routes to professional customers.

These statements describe downstream professional-customer purchase rules. They do **not** prove supplier onboarding terms for brands.

### Source
- https://www.cooperedo.es/cooperedo/

## 3. Hair-tool / dryer category evidence

### Public facts
Cooperedo’s current hairdressing page states that it carries **30+ leading hairdressing brands** and sells professional tools including **hair dryers, straighteners and scissors**.

## 5. Data-governance issue: founding / legal-entity date

### Public-source discrepancy
There is a date inconsistency that should not be silently normalised:
- Cooperedo’s own website says the current business was officially created as Cooperedo S.L. in **1999**.
- Third-party company-registry aggregators currently report legal incorporation of COOPEREDO SL (CIF B12634309) on **27 January 2004**.

Possible explanations include predecessor-business history, restructuring, or a difference between commercial founding and current legal-entity registration, but this has not been verified from an official Mercantile Registry extract.

### Knowledge-base rule
- Use **“business history dates to the 1940s”** as the historical operating fact.
- Do not use either 1999 or 2004 as a definitive current-entity incorporation date without primary registry verification.
