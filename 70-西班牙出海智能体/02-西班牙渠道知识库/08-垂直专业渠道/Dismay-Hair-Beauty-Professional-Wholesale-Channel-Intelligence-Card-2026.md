### Verified EOLUX professional-channel structure — important new fact
On **2026-04-21**, EOLUX officially announced Dismay Hair & Beauty as its **official and exclusive distributor in Spain and Andorra**. EOLUX states that the partnership is intended to strengthen its distribution network and improve service to professional customers and final consumers.

EOLUX's current official `Professional Area` adds a much more specific channel rule:
- salons interested in offering EOLUX are asked to submit a professional form;
- one of EOLUX's **selected professional distributors** then contacts the salon;
- EOLUX explicitly states that it **does not sell directly to salons**.

Taken together, these two brand-side public sources establish a verified professional route for EOLUX in Spain at the structural level:

**EOLUX brand → selected professional distributor layer → salon / professional customer**

and, specifically for Spain/Andorra, Dismay is publicly named as EOLUX's official and exclusive distributor.

Important boundary: the reviewed public sources do **not** prove that every Spanish EOLUX salon order is invoiced, warehoused or serviced directly by Dismay, nor do they disclose whether Dismay uses additional sub-distributors. Those operational details remain To Verify.

Sources:
- EOLUX official announcement, 2026-04-21: https://eolux.com/en-us/blogs/news/eolux-impulsa-crecimiento-espana-y-andorra
- EOLUX official Professional Area: https://eolux.com/en/pages/area-profesional

## 3. AI Analysis — NOT Leon View

### Relevance to 001 AI hair dryer
Dismay remains a high-relevance professional-channel lead because:
1. It is an upstream professional wholesaler/distributor rather than only one retail point.
2. It already commercialises multiple high-speed digital-motor dryer platforms in the **100,000–120,000 rpm** range.
3. Current Dismay listings show that professional competitors already combine high-speed motors with ionic systems, intelligent temperature language, memory functions, lightweight design, filtration/maintenance and multi-accessory propositions.
4. The EOLUX case publicly verifies that a premium hair-device brand can use a selected professional-distributor layer rather than sell directly to salons.

For 001, the following is a **validated reference model for the EOLUX case**, not an assertion about 001's future route:

**Brand → professional distributor → salon / specialist professional customer**

The following remain analytical possibilities only until directly verified with Dismay:
- distribution to specialist retailers beyond the public wholesale description;
- use of regional sub-distributors;
- direct salon fulfilment by Dismay;
- consumer-retail account development performed by Dismay.
