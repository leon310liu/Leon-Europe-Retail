## 1. Public Facts

### Company / channel role
- Dismay Hair & Beauty is based in Badalona, Barcelona.
- Its public positioning is wholesale distribution of hairdressing and beauty products and accessories.
- BeautyMarket's company directory identifies the operating company as **Hair And Beauty Cash And Carry S.L.U.** and describes Dismay as a wholesaler supplying **wholesalers and specialist shops**.
- Dismay's current official website shows a broad professional catalogue including hair electrical products; current top-selling items include hair dryers such as **Secador EX04+ Eolux**, **Twin Secador Wad Professional**, and **Impulse Wad Professional**.

Sources:

- BeautyMarket company directory: https://www.beautymarket.es/peluqueria/dismay-hair-and-beauty-peluqueria-3236-dirempresa.php

### High-speed dryer evidence directly relevant to 001
- Interempresas reported on **2026-05-22** that **EOLUX is distributed by Dismay Hair & Beauty** in the Spanish professional hairdressing context.
- The reported EOLUX Multi-Styler Profesional Iónico uses a **digital high-speed motor at 110,000 rpm**, with **1,875 W** power, ionic technology and magnetic accessories.
- This is direct public evidence that a Spanish professional beauty wholesale/distribution channel is already commercialising the same broad technical territory as 001: high-speed digital-motor hair drying / styling devices.

Source:
- Interempresas, 2026-05-22: https://www.interempresas.net/Peluqueria/632684-EOLUX-presenta-Multi-Styler-Profesional-Ionico-secado-mas-rapido-preciso-respetuoso.html

### Distribution structure signals
- Dismay's public company profile states that it distributes nationally and internationally and has its own delivery service in the Barcelona area.
- Its public LinkedIn company profile states **180+ professional brands** and **1,300+ customers worldwide**. These figures are self-reported company-profile data and should not be treated as audited current operating KPIs.
- The channel appears structurally closer to **B2B wholesaler / distributor-to-distributor / distributor-to-specialist-retailer** than to a direct-to-salon-only retailer.

Source:
- Dismay public LinkedIn company profile: https://www.linkedin.com/company/dismayhairbeauty
