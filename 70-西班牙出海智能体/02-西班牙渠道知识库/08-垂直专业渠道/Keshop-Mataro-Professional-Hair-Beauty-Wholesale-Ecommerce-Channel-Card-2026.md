## 1. Channel identity

### Public facts

- Keshop.com describes itself as the online shop of the Javier Equip salon and says it specialises in professional hair-care and beauty products, with recognised brands and professional stylist advice.
- The website lists its store/contact address as Rda. Alfonso X el Sabio 41, 08304 Mataró, Barcelona.
- Third-party Spanish corporate databases identify the operating legal entity as **KESHOP SL**, CIF **B66842147**, and list its corporate purpose as wholesale and retail sale of hairdressing and cosmetic products, plus manufacture of hairdressing/cosmetic products. The listed principal CNAE is **4645 — wholesale of perfumery and cosmetic products**.
- Keshop states that it has more than 20 professionals from the hairdressing and beauty sector available to advise customers.

### Source quality note

- Keshop website is the primary source for channel positioning, address, service model and logistics.
- Legal-entity/CNAE information is from Spanish business-information databases and should be reconfirmed against Registro Mercantil/BORME before being used in a legal or contractual context.

## 2. Direct relevance to 001 AI hair dryer

### Public facts

Keshop currently lists **WAD Professional Beauty G-Force Pink**, with the following published specifications:

- Brushless motor: **110,000 RPM / 87,000 RPM**
- Ionic function
- 2 speeds
- 4 temperature modes
- Magnetic accessories
- Noise level stated below 78 dB
- Weight stated around 340 g without cable
- Current public product price at time of check: approximately **EUR 104.99 incl. VAT** after discount

This is direct evidence that a 110,000 RPM brushless professional hair dryer is already merchandised by a Spain-based specialist hair/beauty ecommerce operator whose legal activity includes wholesale.

## 3. Geographic reach / fulfilment

### Public facts

Keshop's official shipping page states that it ships to Europe / the European Economic Community.

For Spain it currently publishes, among other options:

- Spain mainland: InPost Point Pack 48/72h; GLS Economy 48/72h; GLS Express 24/48h
- Balearic Islands: InPost / GLS options
- It explicitly states that it **does not ship to Canary Islands, Ceuta or Melilla**
- The same shipping page lists multiple European destinations including Germany, Austria, Belgium, Bulgaria, Denmark and others.

This establishes Keshop as more than a purely local Mataró retail outlet from a fulfilment perspective.

Therefore the knowledge base should use **2016** as the safe public year unless the exact date is verified through Registro Mercantil/BORME.

### Wholesale classification caveat

CNAE / corporate purpose proves that the legal entity is authorised/registered for wholesale activity; it does **not** by itself prove the share of revenue generated from wholesale, nor that every item shown on keshop.com is centrally purchased directly from manufacturers.

## 7. 001 AI hair dryer relevance classification

### Public-fact conclusion

Keshop provides direct market evidence for all of the following:

1. A Spain-based specialist professional hair/beauty operator sells 110,000 RPM brushless hair dryers.
2. The operator's legal activity includes wholesale as well as retail.
3. Its ecommerce fulfilment reaches mainland Spain, Balearics and numerous European markets.
4. High-speed brushless hair dryers are therefore present not only in large electronics chains and premium beauty retailers, but also in specialist professional hair ecommerce / wholesale-capable channels.

### Inference

Keshop is a candidate account worth commercial validation for 001, especially as a specialist hair-channel route rather than a mass-retail route.

This is an **inference**, not a verified supplier-entry rule and not Leon View.
