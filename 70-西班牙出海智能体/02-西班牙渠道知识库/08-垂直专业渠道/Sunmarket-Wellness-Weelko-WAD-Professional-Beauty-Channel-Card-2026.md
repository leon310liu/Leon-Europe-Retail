## 4. What this publicly proves

The public evidence confirms that a specialised Spanish professional beauty / salon-equipment supplier is actively merchandising **110,000 RPM brushless ionic hair dryers**.

Therefore the 110k-RPM BLDC architecture is not limited to mass consumer-electronics chains in Spain; it is also present in the **professional salon / barber / beauty-equipment channel**.

This is a public market fact. It does **not** prove that Sunmarket is the importer of record for these dryers or that it controls the WAD brand in Spain.

## 5. Publicly evidenced channel route

The following downstream route is supported by Sunmarket's own public positioning and sales model:

**professional equipment supplier / distributor → salons, barber shops, beauty centres and other professional operators**

For Weelko-branded / related products, Sunmarket publicly presents Weelko among the brands it distributes, but the precise upstream contractual structure for individual WAD hair-dryer SKUs is not public in the reviewed sources.

## 6. Inference — not public fact

For 001, Sunmarket is a useful benchmark for a possible **professional-beauty distributor route**, distinct from large electrical retailers, department stores or general marketplaces.

Potential model to verify:

**001 / manufacturer or EU importer → specialised professional beauty distributor → salon / barber / beauty-centre accounts**

This is an inference only. It does not establish that Sunmarket accepts new Chinese brands, is seeking another dryer brand, or would list 001.
