## 1. Channel summary

PostQuam is a Spain-based professional cosmetics and hairdressing supplier with a direct B2B-oriented online platform covering hairdressing, beauty, aesthetics, furniture/apparatus and electrical tools. Its official site lists Italian Design among the brands carried on the platform and currently publishes the Italian Design Cyclone Multi Styler 5-in-1, a 110,000 rpm professional hair appliance using the brand's own `Ai-Sensor` wording.

This creates a relevant public-fact benchmark for 001 beyond mass/beauty retail: a Spain-based professional beauty/hairdressing supplier is directly merchandising a sensor-enabled high-speed hair appliance into a professional-oriented B2B environment.

## 5. DATA QUALITY / CONFLICTS / corrections

### Professional reach figure

PostQuam's official history page states `295,000 professionals`. Treat this as a company-published reach/trust claim, not as a verified current active-account count. Exact current number of active B2B purchasing accounts: **TO VERIFY**.

### Founding-year conflict outside the primary source set

Some non-core web pages publish a different founding year for PostQuam. For knowledge-base governance, the current official PostQuam history page is used as the primary source and states **1989**. Alternative years should not be silently merged into the current fact set without documentary support.

### High-speed dryer specification conflict discovered during channel scan

A separate PostQuam SKU, `SECADOR SILENCE ION BLCD`, reference `PQPAE1015`, is titled `110.000 RPM` on PostQuam's dryer-category listing and on at least one reseller page, while the reseller's detailed specification text states **105,000 rpm**. This is a product-data conflict and the SKU should not be used as a clean 110k benchmark until the exact official detailed specification is reconciled.
