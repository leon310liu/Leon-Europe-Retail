## 1. Executive summary

Euronics España is currently one of the clearest public examples of Spain's **central purchasing + associated local retail + omnichannel** model. Its current official corporate page states that Euronics España operates through a **Central de Compras** model and has **more than 400 stores in Spain**. The same page states that Euronics in Spain sits under **Grupo SINERSIS**, whose network totals **1,400 points of sale across all Spanish provinces**.

For the 001 AI hair-dryer project, Euronics is strategically relevant even though this research round did **not** find a currently indexed, official Euronics.es page that independently confirms a 110,000-RPM hair-dryer SKU. Its relevance is therefore channel-structural rather than SKU-confirmed in this card: Euronics gives access to a large specialist appliance/electronics retail network inside SINERSIS and illustrates how a Chinese consumer-electronics or small-appliance supplier may need to think about group-level range decisions, exclusive products and associated-store execution separately.

## 2. Public facts

### 2.1 Euronics España identifies itself as a central-purchasing organisation

Euronics España's current official "Quiénes somos" page states that it operates under a **"modelo de Central de Compras"** and combines national purchasing strength with a network of **more than 400 stores**.

Source: Euronics España, "Quiénes somos" — https://www.euronics.es/quienes-somos.html

### 2.2 Current official network scale

The same official Euronics page gives three current scale markers:

- **+400 Euronics stores in Spain**
- **+11,000 Euronics establishments in Europe**
- **1,400 SINERSIS points of sale in Spain**

It also states that SINERSIS has points of sale in all Spanish provinces.

Source: https://www.euronics.es/quienes-somos.html

### 2.3 Store-count discrepancy must be preserved, not flattened

Euronics' own current website contains different public store-count formulations:

- "Quiénes somos": **+400 tiendas en España**
- Store locator: "una red de unas **400 tiendas físicas**"
- "Confianza" page: "más de **300 tiendas** en toda España"

These pages are all current-access official Euronics pages, so the knowledge base should not silently convert them into one false precision number. The safest current representation is **approximately 400 / 400+ stores**, while retaining the lower "300+" wording as a live-site inconsistency.

Sources:
- https://www.euronics.es/quienes-somos.html
- https://www.euronics.es/tiendas/localizador
- https://www.euronics.es/confianza.html

### 2.4 Industry data gives a lower 2024 point-of-sale count

Electromarket's 2024-2025 sector report lists Euronics under SINERSIS with **382 points of sale in 2023 and 367 in 2024**. The same table lists Tien21 at 613 stores and Milar at 377 stores for 2024.

This is not necessarily a contradiction with the current Euronics "400+" statement because the dates and counting methodology may differ. It should remain a dated historical industry datapoint rather than replace the current official statement.

Source: Electromarket sector report 2024-2025 — https://www.electromarket.com/revista/372/45/

### 2.5 SINERSIS is actively using group-wide exclusive-brand/product strategy

On 5 June 2026, Electromarket reported that SINERSIS incorporated **HOTPOINT and OTSEIN** into its catalogue of exclusive brands/products for distribution across the group and specifically through **Euronics, Milar and Tien21**. The article states that this is part of SINERSIS' strategy around exclusive products, own brands and TES.

This is important public evidence that range architecture can be managed at group level across multiple banners, rather than only store-by-store.

Source: Electromarket, 2026-06-05 — https://www.electromarket.com/noticia/33736/sinersis-incorpora-hotpoint-y-otsein-a-su-catalogo-de-marcas-exclusiv.html

### 2.6 Omnichannel and local-store execution

Euronics' official site states that it supports:

- Click & Collect
- professional installation
- financing
- removal/recycling of old appliances
- online ordering linked to local-store availability and postcode

The site describes an omnichannel model intended to combine digital purchasing with local specialist-store service.

Sources:
- https://www.euronics.es/quienes-somos.html
- https://www.euronics.es/
- https://www.euronics.es/confianza.html

## 3. Channel structure — analysis / inference, NOT public fact

Based on the public facts above, a practical structural interpretation for market-entry analysis is:

**Brand / manufacturer / importer → SINERSIS / Euronics central purchasing or group assortment decision → associated/local Euronics stores + Euronics.es → consumer**

For products selected as group-wide exclusive or strategic references, another plausible structure is:

**Brand / manufacturer → SINERSIS group-level agreement → Euronics + Milar + Tien21 banners → associated stores / online channels → consumer**

This is an **analysis/inference** built from public evidence about central purchasing, SINERSIS ownership/network and the 2026 group-wide HOTPOINT/OTSEIN rollout. It is not a claim that every SKU follows this exact flow.

## 4. Relevance to 001 AI Hair Dryer

### Publicly supported relevance

- It operates a central-purchasing model.
- It sits inside SINERSIS, which controls a much broader multi-banner network.
- SINERSIS has demonstrated in 2026 that it can introduce brands/products across Euronics, Milar and Tien21 at group level.

### Not verified in this research round

## 5. Entry-rule implications — analysis / inference

For a new consumer-electronics or personal-care appliance brand, Euronics/SINERSIS may require two separate questions:

1. **Can the product be accepted at central/group assortment level?**
2. **How is that assortment activated across associated stores and online fulfilment?**

The 2026 HOTPOINT/OTSEIN example indicates that group-level exclusive-brand/product agreements are operationally real. However, public sources reviewed here do not disclose the actual supplier onboarding workflow, listing fee, margin requirement, MOQ, payment term, rebate, sell-through obligation or whether individual associated stores retain meaningful discretion over non-exclusive ranges.

These points remain TO VERIFY.
