# Spain ElectroHogar Buying Groups — 2025 PAE Revenue & Store Network Benchmark

> Scope: public-fact knowledge for Spain consumer electronics / small domestic appliances (PAE) channel mapping.
> Updated: 2026-09-03
> Classification: PUBLIC FACTS + clearly labelled observations. No Leon View added.

## 1. Why this card matters

Electromarket's *Electroguía 2025-26* provides a comparable 2025 benchmark for Spain's specialist ElectroHogar buying groups, including total sales, PAE turnover and store-network evolution. This is useful for separating:

- group-level commercial scale,
- PAE / small-appliance relevance,
- consumer-facing banner store count,
- associate/member count.

These metrics should not be treated as interchangeable.

## 4. PUBLIC FACTS — associate/member count

Electromarket reports for 2025:

- Sinersis: **16 members** (unchanged vs 2024).
- Segesa: **14 members** (13 in 2024).
- Cadena Elecco: **4 associated companies** (5 in 2024).

This is a separate metric from store count and should not be used as a proxy for the number of purchasing doors.

## 5. 001 AI hair-dryer relevance — factual boundary

PAE (Pequeño Aparato Electrodoméstico / small domestic appliances) is the relevant category bucket for hair dryers in these buying-group datasets.

The 2025 Electromarket figures show:

- Sinersis reported **€103.2m PAE turnover**.
- Segesa reported **€28.09m PAE turnover**.

These figures establish category scale, but **do not prove that a specific hair-dryer supplier can access central buying**, and they do not reveal buyer names, listing fees, MOQ, margin requirements or whether individual SKU decisions are fully centralised.

Those fields remain **TO VERIFY** at group/platform level.

## 6. Data-governance update

Use four separate fields in future Channel Cards:

1. `Group turnover`
2. `PAE turnover`
3. `Consumer-facing stores / POS`
4. `Associated companies / regional platforms`

Do not merge these figures into a generic “channel size”.

### Conflict / ageing flags

- `SINERSIS 1,500+ stores` — historical/other-date public claim; do not treat as current 2025 banner count.
- `SINERSIS 2025 reported banner total` — 1,264 = Tien21 555 + Milar 366 + Euronics 343.
- `SEGESA 2025 Cadena Redder` — 1,244 POS.
- Different sources may use “stores”, “points of sale”, “members”, “associates”, “platforms” or “warehouses”; retain the original metric name.

## 8. Inference / Leon View boundary

### PUBLIC-FACT-SUPPORTED OBSERVATION

Sinersis is materially larger than Segesa in reported 2025 PAE turnover, while its reported Tien21/Milar/Euronics store network contracted during the same period. This is a descriptive comparison of published data only.

### NOT ESTABLISHED BY PUBLIC DATA

The sources do **not** establish:

- that Sinersis is necessarily easier or harder for 001 to enter;
- that PAE procurement is completely centralised;
- that all 1,264 reported Sinersis-banner stores carry hair dryers;
- that Cadena Redder's 1,244 POS have uniform assortment or purchasing authority;
- any recommended entry sequence for 001.
