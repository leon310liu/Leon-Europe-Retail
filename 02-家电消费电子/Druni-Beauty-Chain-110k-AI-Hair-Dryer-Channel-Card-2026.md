## 2. Public facts — corporate and store scale

### Druni official current profile

Druni’s official corporate page states that the company was founded in Carlet (Valencia) in 1987 and currently has **more than 300 stores** and **more than 2,000 employees in Spain**. It describes itself as a specialist chain in perfume, cosmetics, make-up and personal care, and notes that its ecommerce operation launched in 2015.

Source: Druni official — “Quiénes somos”
https://www.druni.es/quienes-somos

### Group ownership / Iberian beauty platform

MC / Sonae completed the combination of Druni and Arenal in **July 2024**. The resulting group is held **50% by MC and 50% by Druni’s founding shareholders**. MC states that the transaction created the leading specialized health, wellness and beauty retail group in Iberia.

Source: MC / Sonae official, 2024 transaction announcement
https://mc.sonae.pt/mc-sonae-2/mc-torna-se-lider-em-retalho-de-saude-beleza-e-bem-estar-na-peninsula-iberica/

MC’s 2024 annual report states that the Druni partnership created an Iberian health, wellness and beauty platform with **more than 750 stores** across Druni, Arenal and Wells, together with a strong online channel. It separately describes Druni as having **more than 400 stores in Spain** at the time of the report.

Source: MC Annual Report 2024
https://mc.sonae.pt/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/MC_RC2024_final_v2.pdf

### Data-governance note: store-count conflict

Current Druni official website: **300+ stores**.
MC Annual Report 2024: **400+ Druni stores in Spain**.

These figures conflict and may reflect different dates, definitions, banners, counting methodology or stale website copy. Do **not** overwrite one with the other. For current channel sizing, retain both with source/date labels and mark exact 2026 Druni store count **TO VERIFY**.

Source: Druni product page
https://www.druni.es/cyclone-multi-styler-iditalian-secador-profesional-5-en-1

This is direct evidence that the Spanish specialist beauty-retail channel is already merchandising a product with the combination:

`110k high-speed motor + intelligent sensing/automatic adjustment + multi-styling attachments`

This is materially closer to the 001 AI Hair Dryer proposition than generic ionic or brushless hair dryers.

## 5. Channel structure — fact vs inference

### Public facts

- Druni is a major Spanish specialized beauty/perfumery retailer with a national store network and ecommerce.
- Druni currently lists a 110k rpm product explicitly marketed with AI-Sensor functionality.
- Druni is part of the Druni/Arenal partnership under the wider MC/Sonae health, wellness and beauty portfolio.
- MC explicitly identifies store-network expansion and ecommerce growth for Druni/Arenal/Wells as a strategic pillar.

Source: MC strategic pillars
https://mc.sonae.pt/en/strategic-pillars/

### Structured inference — NOT a public fact

Potential route for a brand in this channel may be:

`Brand / Spanish importer or distributor → Druni commercial/category team → ecommerce and/or store assortment → consumer`

This route is a **structured inference only**. Public sources reviewed in this run do not disclose whether the specific Cyclone SKU is:

- directly purchased by Druni central buying,
- supplied via a Spanish distributor,
- drop-shipped,
- stocked in all stores,
- online-only,
- selectively stocked by region.

Therefore none of those should be written as facts.
