# Dubal Cosmetics × BaBylissPRO — Professional Hair & Beauty Wholesale Channel Card (Spain, 2026)

> Fact-only research card. No Leon View has been added or inferred.
> Capture date: 2026-09-03.

## 1. Channel identity

**Entity:** Dubal Cosmetics S.L.

**Channel classification:** Professional Hair & Beauty Distributor / Wholesaler + specialist retail network.

### Public facts
- Dubal Cosmetics states that it has **more than 40 years of experience** distributing professional hairdressing products in Catalonia and across Spain, excluding Ceuta and Melilla.
- Its target professional customers explicitly include **peluquerías, barberías and centros de estética**.
- It describes itself as a **mayorista / distribuidor oficial** and offers professional customers **exclusive professional pricing / personalized wholesale tariffs**, technical advice and commercial visits in Catalonia.
- Its catalogue includes professional hair cosmetics, colouring, electrical tools, specialised utensils, furniture and aesthetic products.
- It states that it distributes **40+ professional brands**.
- The company also operates proprietary/exclusive professional lines, including **Myssue**, and has a separate Dubal-branded product catalogue.

Source: https://www.dubalcosmetics.com/content/17-distribuidores
