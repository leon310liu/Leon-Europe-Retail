## 1. Channel identity

Miravia operates a Spain-facing marketplace with at least two seller-facing models explicitly presented on its own recruitment pages:

- **Brand sellers / official stores (品牌卖家 / Tienda oficial)**
- **Ordinary / marketplace sellers (普通卖家 / Marketplace seller)**

Miravia's China-facing official recruitment site states that both brand sellers and ordinary sellers can apply, that there is **no annual fee**, commissions are charged on sales, account managers are provided, and sellers can use Miravia's **DBM official logistics service**.

Source: Miravia official seller recruitment site: https://cnseller.miravia.es/

Miravia's Spanish seller FAQ states that seller onboarding includes business validation and that documentation differs depending on whether the seller applies for a marketplace store or an official store. It also states that Miravia evaluates whether products fit the platform and reviews the applicant's online-selling experience.

Source: Miravia official seller FAQ: https://sell.miravia.es/faq

## 2. Entry economics — public rules

Miravia's official seller FAQ states:

- no fixed cost to list the catalogue;
- commission is charged on sales;
- commission range is **6%–15% depending on category and seller type**;
- official stores can carry higher commissions than marketplace stores;
- onboarding includes business validation and brand registration / brand protection steps.

Source: https://sell.miravia.es/faq

Miravia's official fee page (currently published as the **2025 commission table**) lists **Belleza 13%**, with subcategories including **Cuidado personal 11%**. Because this table is explicitly labelled 2025, it must **not** be represented as a guaranteed 2026 tariff without Seller Center confirmation.

Source: https://sell.miravia.es/feelist

**Data-governance status:**
- General commission range 6%–15%: current official FAQ.
- Exact 2026 hair-dryer commission: **TO VERIFY IN CURRENT SELLER CENTER / CONTRACT**.

## 3. Product identification / catalogue requirement

GS1 Spain's Miravia marketplace guidance states that Miravia product publication generally requires an **EAN / GTIN-13**, and that onboarding also requires basic company documentation such as bank certificates, tax/company documents and company-creation documentation.

Source: GS1 Spain: https://www.gs1es.org/venta-en-marketplaces/miravia/

This is especially relevant for new consumer-electronics brands because GTIN/EAN readiness should be treated as an onboarding prerequisite rather than a later merchandising task.

## 5. Publicly supported channel structure

### Route A — Official brand store

Brand / authorized brand entity → Miravia official-store onboarding → Miravia catalogue + optional DBM logistics → Spanish consumer

### Route B — Marketplace seller

Brand / distributor / qualified seller → Miravia marketplace account → seller-operated storefront / catalogue → seller or Miravia-supported logistics → Spanish consumer

### Route C — China / cross-border seller recruitment

Miravia's official China recruitment site explicitly targets both brand sellers and ordinary sellers with supply-chain and operating capabilities and promotes **DBM official logistics**.

China / cross-border seller → Miravia seller validation → Spain-facing listing → DBM or other permitted fulfilment configuration → Spanish consumer

## 6. What the evidence does NOT prove

The public evidence above does **not** prove that:

- Miravia buys these dryers as 1P wholesale inventory;
- official-store status means Miravia owns the stock;
- a brand can enter without legal / business verification;
- DBM automatically includes warehousing, customs representation or importer-of-record responsibility for every seller;
- the 2025 fee table remains unchanged in 2026;
- any 110k dryer listed on Miravia is stocked in Spanish physical retail;
- Miravia has approved 001 or any related supplier.

These must remain separate from verified facts.

## 8. Knowledge-base governance note

Miravia should **not** be modelled as equivalent to MediaMarkt 1P retail, Sinersis regional purchasing groups, or a traditional wholesaler. Public evidence supports modelling it as a **marketplace platform with differentiated official-store and marketplace-seller tracks plus platform-supported logistics**.

Also avoid collapsing **Tienda oficial** and **Vendido por third-party seller** into a single seller-status field; seller identity and store type must be stored separately at SKU level.
