## 4. Channel-role classification

### PUBLIC FACT

Dismay is structurally different from a retail salon-supply shop:
- it explicitly identifies as wholesale;
- it supplies wholesalers/distributors and specialist stores;
- it distributes brands nationally and internationally;
- it has a Cash & Carry / wholesale operating model in public company descriptions;
- it is a distributor of WAD Professional Beauty, including the 110k G-Force platform.

### INFERENCE — NOT LEON VIEW

For 001, Dismay should be modeled as an **upstream professional hair/beauty wholesale distribution node**, not merely as a professional ecommerce retailer.

Candidate route:

`Brand / OEM / EU-Spain brand owner → national professional wholesaler/distributor (Dismay-type node) → regional wholesalers / distributors / specialist beauty stores / salon-supply retailers → peluquerías / barberías / professional users`

This is one layer upstream from channels such as System Forme or other professional salon-equipment retailers.

This structure is directly relevant to 001 because it demonstrates a proven Spanish professional-wholesale route for a 110,000 rpm brushless dryer brand.
