## 1. Public facts

### Expert Spain network

Expert Spain states that it is a chain of associated stores specializing in appliances, IT and telephony, with more than 30 years of experience and around 350 establishments across Spain.

Source: https://www.expert.es/la-empresa

### Logistics / regional platform structure

Expert Spain states that 7 logistics platforms serve its points of sale daily. The same official page identifies regional/logistics entities including:

- Unió Electrodoméstica Balear, S.A. (UNEBSA Cataluña) — Castellbisbal, Barcelona
- Unió Electrodoméstica Balear, S.A. (UNEBSA Baleares) — Palma de Mallorca
- Joaquín Fernández S.A. (Grupo Jofer) — Sevilla
- CAYPRE, S.L. — Alicante
- Agrupación Canaria de Electrodomésticos, S.A. (ACADESA Tenerife) — El Rosario, Tenerife
- Agrupación Canaria de Electrodomésticos, S.A. (ACADESA Las Palmas) — Agüimes, Gran Canaria

The official page says there are 7 platforms, but the currently rendered company list contains duplicated entries and does not cleanly expose seven distinct legal entities. Do not infer a missing seventh entity without another source.

Source: https://www.expert.es/la-empresa

### Associated / independent retailer model

Expert Spain explicitly describes its points of sale as independent and offers a public application process for retailers that want to join the network. The application asks for applicant identity, store location, telephone and e-mail.

Source: https://www.expert.es/la-empresa
