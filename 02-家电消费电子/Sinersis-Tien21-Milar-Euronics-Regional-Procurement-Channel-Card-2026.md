# Sinersis × Tien21 / Milar / Euronics — Regional Procurement Channel Card (Spain, 2026)

> Status: PUBLIC FACT research card  
> Updated: 2026-09-11  
> Scope: Spain consumer electronics / electrical-appliance specialist channel; relevant to 001 AI hair-dryer route research.  
> Rule: PUBLIC FACT, INFERENCE and LEON VIEW are kept separate. No Leon View is created in this card.

## 1. Why this channel matters

Sinersis is not adequately modeled as a single national retail chain. Its own current materials describe a service / coordination organization connected to regional associated platforms and the retail banners Euronics, Milar and Tien21. For a new consumer-electronics brand, the regional-platform layer is therefore a material channel fact that must be researched separately from the consumer-facing banners.

## 2. PUBLIC FACTS — group and procurement structure

### 2.1 Sinersis central role

Sinersis' current homepage states that it coordinates the commercial interests of regional associated platforms. It says its role includes directing banner marketing strategies and negotiating framework and promotional agreements with suppliers ("negociación de los acuerdos marco y promocionales con los proveedores"). It also describes the organization as having evolved from a buying group into a sales organization in the electrical-appliance market.

Source: Sinersis homepage, accessed 2026-09-11: https://www.sinersis.es/

### 2.2 Regional platforms perform purchasing and logistics functions

The current "Asociados" page states that Sinersis' associated regional platforms manage logistics for more than 1,500 points of sale and operate 19 warehouses across Spain for procurement / supply and logistics in their areas of influence. Services explicitly listed for associates include:

- purchasing management (Gestión de compras)
- full point-of-sale development
- brand-image implementation
- space and shelf optimization advice
- promotional communication campaigns
- marketing campaigns
- logistics optimization
- training for points of sale

Source: Sinersis — Asociados, accessed 2026-09-11: https://www.sinersis.es/asociados/

### 2.3 Named regional platforms are a real operational layer

The same official page lists regional organizations including Caslesa, Cedecasa, Celsa, CODECO, COMELSA, DIVELSA, GRUDESA, Idea Hogar, L. Roman, Medired, Proselco, Sacse, Sorefoz, TIANELSUR, Ugesa, Vere and Vicosa.

For 001's central-Spain research, two examples are particularly relevant:

- **Vere (Grupo Vere 85, S.A.)** — official Sinersis profile places it in Cazalegas, Toledo, and describes its commercial activity across part of Castilla-La Mancha, Extremadura and Valle del Tiétar, commercially identified with Tien21.
- **Proselco** — official Sinersis profile places it in Colmenar Viejo, Madrid and says it develops the Tien21 and Milar banners and provides integrated service to associated points of sale.

Sources:  
https://www.sinersis.es/asociado/vere/  
https://www.sinersis.es/asociado/proselco/

### 2.4 Evidence that regional members can themselves be buying centers

Official Sinersis profiles provide concrete examples:

- **UGESA** describes itself as a reference buying center ("central de compras") for appliances and consumer electronics in the northwest, with more than 38 members and more than 150 associated businesses representing Tien21 and Euronics in Galicia, Asturias and León.
- **COMELSA** describes itself as a leading buying center in appliance distribution, operating with Milar and with presence in Comunidad Valenciana, Albacete, Teruel, Murcia and Baleares.
- **GRUDESA** states that it originated from small Murcia retailers joining forces to make joint purchases and obtain better commercial terms.

Sources:  
https://www.sinersis.es/asociado/ugesa/  
https://www.sinersis.es/asociado/comelsa/  
https://www.sinersis.es/asociado/grudesa/

### 2.5 Group-wide brand distribution is possible

Electromarket reported on 2026-06-05 that Sinersis added HOTPOINT and OTSEIN to its exclusive-brand catalogue and that both brands would be distributed throughout the group and its Euronics, Milar and Tien21 banners. This is public evidence that at least some brand agreements can be activated at group-wide scale.

Source: Electromarket, 2026-06-05: https://www.electromarket.com/noticia/33736/sinersis-incorpora-hotpoint-y-otsein-a-su-catalogo-de-marcas-exclusiv.html

## 3. PUBLIC DATA CONFLICT / GOVERNANCE

### 3.1 16 vs 17 regional platforms on Sinersis' own current website

There is an unresolved internal inconsistency as of 2026-09-11:

- Sinersis homepage says **17 regional associated platforms**.
- Sinersis "Asociados" page says **16 associated platforms** in its headline/body, while the visible directory lists 17 names including Vicosa.

Status: **CONFLICT / TO VERIFY**. Do not normalize 16 or 17 into a single supposedly exact current number without further confirmation.

Sources:  
https://www.sinersis.es/  
https://www.sinersis.es/asociados/

### 3.2 Point-of-sale counts vary materially by date and scope

Public sources currently expose several different figures:

- Sinersis "Asociados" page: regional associates manage logistics for **more than 1,500 points of sale** (current page, accessed 2026-09-11).
- Sinersis republished October 2024 material: **1,563 stores** and 16 logistics platforms at that historical point.
- Electromarket, 2026-09-07: refers to Sinersis' **1,400 points of sale** when reporting its new marketing director.
- Electromarket's 2025-2026 sector report gives 2025 banner-level figures of **Tien21 555, Milar 366, Euronics 343** (1,264 combined in that table), following network reorganization.

These numbers are not assumed to use the same perimeter (stores vs points of sale, banners included, associated network vs report sample, timing). Status: **CONFLICT / SCOPE TO VERIFY**.

## 4. 2026 network change worth tracking

Electromarket reported on 2025-12-19 that Grupo Medired would join Sinersis from **2026-01-01**. Sinersis' current associated-platform page now includes Medired, and Medired's official Sinersis profile says it has a network in Comunidad Valenciana, Murcia, Albacete, Baleares, Teruel and Soria.

This is evidence that the regional platform structure is not static and historic network counts can become obsolete quickly.

## 6. INFERENCE — research model, not an official organization chart

Based on the verified public roles above, a useful hypothesis for future validation is:

**Brand / Spanish importer → Sinersis-level framework or promotional agreement and/or regional associated buying platform → Tien21 / Milar / Euronics associated points of sale → consumer**

This is explicitly an **INFERENCE**. The exact decision rights between Sinersis central, each regional platform and individual stores may vary by brand, category, campaign and commercial agreement.
