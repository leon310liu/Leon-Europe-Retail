## 1. Why this channel matters for 001 AI Hair Dryer

Perfumerías Primor is a large Spain-origin beauty/perfumery specialist retailer rather than a traditional appliance chain. Its current Spanish ecommerce directly lists a Perfect Beauty professional 6-in-1 hair dryer with a **BLDC 110,000 rpm motor**. This is direct evidence that 110k high-speed hair appliances are already accepted inside a mainstream beauty-specialist retail environment in Spain.

For 001, this adds a distinct route alongside appliance/electronics chains, marketplaces, IT distributors and professional salon distributors.

## 3. PUBLIC FACT — Primor ecommerce is a direct retail / 1P sales model

Primor's current General Terms state that purchases on primor.eu for Peninsular Spain, Balearic Islands and Andorra are sold by **ROSA CREMA, S.L. (CIF B92683259)**.

The same terms state:
- PRIMOR focuses on sale of the products offered on its website;
- product prices are set on Primor's platforms;
- Primor may limit quantities because products are sold at retail for personal use;
- ecommerce and physical stores are treated as separate sales channels;
- Primor manages stock availability, shipment and order fulfilment from its warehouse / preparation network.

This supports classifying the Spanish Primor ecommerce listing as **direct Primor retail (1P)** rather than an open third-party marketplace listing.

Source:
- Primor official — Condiciones generales: https://www.primor.eu/es_es/condiciones-generales

Important boundary:
- This proves the legal seller for Primor's Spanish ecommerce is ROSA CREMA, S.L.
- It does **not** prove that every ecommerce SKU is physically stocked by every Primor store.
- Primor explicitly states online and physical-store channels are independent and prices need not match.
