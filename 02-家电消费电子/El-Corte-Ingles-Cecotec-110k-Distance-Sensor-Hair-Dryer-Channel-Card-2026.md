## 1. Why this matters for 001

El Corte Inglés (ECI) now has a live product page for Cecotec DryGlam Waves Sensor 6in1, a 110,000 rpm BLDC hair-styling/drying product using a distance sensor to automatically adjust heat according to proximity to hair. This is direct public evidence that a major Spanish premium omnichannel retailer is presenting a high-speed, sensor-controlled hair appliance to Spanish consumers.

This does **not** prove ECI central 1P buying, nor does it prove an ECI buyer has approved the broader category of “AI hair dryers.” The public page does not expose enough seller/contract information to make that claim.
