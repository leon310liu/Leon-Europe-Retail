# CODECO / Milar Andalucía — Sinersis Regional Distribution Channel Card — 2026

Last verified: 2026-08-29

## 1. Scope

This card records only publicly verifiable facts about CODECO / Milar Andalucía that are relevant to consumer electronics / SDA brands and especially project 001 AI hair dryer. It separates public facts, AI analysis, open questions, and Leon View. No Leon View is created in this document.

## 2. Public facts — organizational position

### 2.1 CODECO is the regional Milar distribution central for Andalucía

Sinersis states that CODECO has been integrated into Sinersis since 2006 and is the distribution central for the Milar brand in Andalucía.

Sinersis currently states that CODECO has more than 60 points of sale and is present in Córdoba, Jaén, Sevilla, Cádiz, Granada, Málaga and Ceuta.

Source:
- Sinersis — CODECO associate profile: https://www.sinersis.es/asociado/codeco/

### 2.2 CODECO remains commercially active in 2025–2026

Ei Actualidad's Andalucía sector roundtable published on 2025-12-02 includes CODECO (Sinersis) as an active participant.

A 2025-10-03 industry article also describes Milar in Andalucía as managed by CODECO and refers to more than 60 stores in the region.

Sources:
- Ei Actualidad — Tertulia Comunidad de Andalucía, 2025-12-02: https://electro-imagen.com/public/index.php/articulo/tertulia-comunidad-de-andalucia

## 3. Public facts — regional operating model

### 3.1 Daily store replenishment is explicitly described

Sinersis states that CODECO optimizes delivery routes so that Milar stores receive daily supply. Sinersis explicitly links this model to faster stock rotation and reduced need for stores to hold inventory.

This is a material public fact for route-to-market modelling because CODECO is not only a branding layer; it performs an active regional logistics function.

Source:
- https://www.sinersis.es/asociado/codeco/

### 3.2 National marketing is adapted regionally by CODECO

Sinersis states that the national marketing plan set by Sinersis is complemented and adapted by CODECO for the commercial influence zones of its Milar stores, with offline and online advertising coordinated centrally at CODECO level.

Source:
- https://www.sinersis.es/asociado/codeco/

### 3.3 CODECO runs supplier-facing Milar trade events

CODECO's official Jornadas Milar site states that the event is an opportunity for brands to present new products, product ranges and exclusive purchasing conditions to Milar points of sale in Andalucía, Ceuta and Melilla.

The site also describes CODECO as the central organization for Milar stores in Andalucía and states that it has 60 branded establishments in Andalucía and Ceuta.

Source:
- CODECO / Jornadas Milar: https://jornadasmilar.codeco.es/jornadas-milar/
- Main event site: https://jornadasmilar.codeco.es/

Publicly visible contact on the event site:
- marketing@codeco.es

Important boundary:
- The public event wording proves that supplier presentations and exclusive purchase-condition discussions occur in this regional ecosystem.
- It does NOT by itself prove that CODECO has unilateral final listing authority for every PAE SKU or that a supplier can enter Milar Andalucía simply by participating in the event.

## 6. AI Analysis — not Leon View

1. CODECO should be modelled as an operational regional platform, not merely as a store-count label: public sources confirm logistics, regional marketing execution, store support and supplier-facing events.
2. For project 001, the existence of current AI / digital hair dryers in Milar establishes category-format compatibility at the Milar ecosystem level.
3. CODECO's supplier event is a potentially relevant discovery / presentation point because brands are explicitly invited to present new products, ranges and purchase conditions to the regional store network.
4. This evidence is not sufficient to conclude that CODECO alone can approve a new AI hair-dryer listing, nor that a regional pilot can bypass Sinersis national agreements.
5. Therefore supplier-entry logic should remain split into verified public structure and unverified commercial authority.

## 7. Data quality and conflicts

### 7.1 Store-count wording

Current Sinersis profile:
- “more than 60 points of sale”.

CODECO Jornadas Milar site:
- “60 establishments identified with the brand in Andalucía and Ceuta”.

Ei Actualidad, 2025-10-03:
- “more than 60 stores”.

These are close but not identical public counts. They should be retained as source-specific wording rather than normalized into an exact 2026 store count without a dated official directory export.

### 7.2 Geographic wording

Sinersis' CODECO profile names Andalucía provinces plus Ceuta.
CODECO's Jornadas Milar event text says the event serves Milar points of sale in Andalucía, Ceuta and Melilla.

This does not prove that CODECO operates a permanent Milar distribution network in Melilla under the same exact structure. Melilla should therefore remain a scope item to verify separately.

## 8. Open verification queue

Priority questions for future research / direct outreach:

- Exact 2026 number and directory of CODECO-linked Milar stores.
- Whether Melilla is permanently served by CODECO or only included in event participation / commercial coverage.
- Which PAE / personal-care suppliers are contracted directly by Sinersis versus negotiated or executed at CODECO level.
- Final SKU-listing authority for hair dryers and other cuidado personal products.
- Whether CODECO can run an Andalucía-only or selected-store pilot for a new brand.
- Buyer / category-manager contact for PAE / cuidado personal.
- Supplier onboarding documentation and commercial process.
- MOQ / minimum order value.
- Payment terms.
- Rebates, marketing contributions, logistics deductions and returns policy.
- Warehouse location, capacity and receiving rules.
- Whether regional warehouse stock is centrally owned by CODECO or follows another ownership model.
- Importer of Record requirements for non-EU brands.
- WEEE / RAEE, packaging EPR and battery responsibilities expected from suppliers.
- SAT, spare-parts, DOA and warranty-service requirements for hair dryers.
- Relationship between Milar national ecommerce assortment and CODECO regional physical-store stocking decisions.
