# Sinersis × 17 Regional Platforms × Euronics / Milar / Tien21 — Channel Card (Spain, 2026)

> Knowledge type: PUBLIC FACT + clearly labelled INFERENCE / TO VERIFY  
> Updated: 2026-09-10  
> Scope: Spain home appliances / consumer electronics distribution and procurement structure  
> Leon View: **NONE ADDED**

## 1. Why this matters

Sinersis should not be modelled simply as a single national retail chain. Its own current public materials describe a layered structure in which Sinersis acts as a central services / commercial-coordination organization for **17 associated regional platforms**, while the consumer-facing network operates under **Tien21, Milar and Euronics**.

For a China-to-Spain consumer-electronics market-entry knowledge base, this creates a distinct channel layer between a national commercial agreement and the local retail store network.

---

## 2. PUBLIC FACT — Sinersis central role

Sinersis states that it is the organization that **coordinates the commercial interests of 17 associated regional platforms**. It says its role includes:

- directing marketing strategies for its retail banners; and
- negotiating **framework agreements and promotional agreements with suppliers** ("acuerdos marco y promocionales con los proveedores").

Sinersis also describes its evolution from a purchasing group ("grupo de compras") into a sales organization in the Spanish household-appliance market.

Current official source:
- Sinersis homepage: https://www.sinersis.es/ (checked 2026-09-10)

### Research implication — INFERENCE, not official org chart

A useful working model for supplier research is:

**Supplier / Brand → Sinersis national commercial-framework layer → one or more regional associated platforms → Tien21 / Milar / Euronics stores → Consumer**

This is an **INFERENCE based on the roles Sinersis publicly describes**. It is not proof that every SKU, every purchase order, every invoice or every replenishment decision follows this exact path.

PO issuer, invoice entity, assortment authority, regional autonomy and SKU-level ordering rules remain **TO VERIFY**.

---

## 3. PUBLIC FACT — 17 regional logistics platforms

Sinersis states that its **17 associates** form Spain's broadest specialized household-appliance logistics network ("red ... de logística especializada en electrodomésticos").

Official source:
- Sinersis homepage / Asociados section: https://www.sinersis.es/ (checked 2026-09-10)

This regional-platform layer is strategically important because individual associates can have their own geographic footprint, warehouses, member stores and local service structures.

### Confirmed examples

#### UGESA — Northwest

Sinersis' official UGESA profile states that Unión Gallega de Electrodomésticos S.A. is a purchasing center for household appliances and consumer electronics in northwest Spain. It reports:

- more than 38 socios;
- more than 150 asociados;
- coverage of Galicia, Asturias and León;
- stores under Tien21 and Euronics.

Source:
- https://www.sinersis.es/asociado/ugesa/ (checked 2026-09-10)

#### COMELSA — East / Southeast footprint

Sinersis' official COMELSA profile describes COMELSA as a purchasing center in appliance distribution with activity in:

- Comunidad Valenciana;
- Albacete;
- Teruel;
- Murcia;
- Baleares.

It reports **more than 90 stores globally** and operates with the Milar banner.

Source:
- https://www.sinersis.es/asociado/comelsa/ (checked 2026-09-10)

#### DIVELSA

Sinersis' official DIVELSA profile reports:

- more than 150 points of sale, combining owned and associated stores;
- more than 20,000 m² of own warehouse space;
- activity through Euronics and Tien21.

Source:
- https://www.sinersis.es/asociado/divelsa/ (checked 2026-09-10)

These examples confirm that the "regional platform" layer is operationally material and is not merely a marketing subdivision.

## 4. PUBLIC FACT — consumer-facing banners and current scale claims

Sinersis' current homepage presents three retail banners:

- Tien21
- Milar
- Euronics

The same page currently claims **more than 1,500 stores in Spain** across its brands and **more than €1 billion in aggregate turnover**.

Source:
- https://www.sinersis.es/ (checked 2026-09-10)

Sinersis also identifies itself as a member of Euronics International.

## 5. DATA GOVERNANCE — current official figures conflict

Do **not** treat a single store-count figure as settled without a dated source.

### Conflict A — Euronics Spain store count

Current Sinersis homepage presents banner-level counters that include **380 Euronics stores** (within the three-brand counters shown on the page), while the current Euronics Spain "Quiénes somos" page states **more than 400 stores in Spain**.

Separately, a Sinersis press release dated 2025-01-07 referred to **more than 300 Euronics stores in Spain** during the Star Days campaign.

Sources:
- https://www.sinersis.es/ — current counters, checked 2026-09-10
- https://www.euronics.es/quienes-somos.html — current claim of +400 stores, checked 2026-09-10
- https://www.sinersis.es/comienza-la-campana-star-days-de-euronics/ — dated 2025-01-07

Status: **CONFLICT / TO VERIFY**. Possible causes include different counting dates, banner migrations, inclusion/exclusion criteria or stale web copy. No cause should be asserted without evidence.

### Conflict B — Sinersis group point-of-sale count

Sinersis' current homepage says **more than 1,500 stores** across its brands, while Euronics Spain's current "Quiénes somos" page states **1,400 points of sale of Grupo Sinersis**.

Sources:
- https://www.sinersis.es/
- https://www.euronics.es/quienes-somos.html

Status: **CONFLICT / TO VERIFY**.

Use **"1,400–1,500+ depending on source/date" only as a research note**, not as a definitive current count in external-facing outputs.

## 6. PUBLIC FACT — online/offline network behavior

Euronics Spain's current general terms explicitly allow online orders to be collected at a selected Euronics physical store. It states that the customer selects a store during checkout and receives a collection code when the order is ready.

Source:
- https://www.euronics.es/condiciones-generales.html (checked 2026-09-10)

Euronics' help center also states that not every online SKU is stocked in every physical store, and customers must contact the individual store to confirm local physical inventory.

Source:
- https://ayuda.euronics.es/support/solutions/articles/23000027668--tienen-este-producto-en-tienda-f%C3%ADsica- (modified 2025-01-30; checked 2026-09-10)

### Research implication — INFERENCE

This supports treating Euronics Spain as an **omnichannel network with meaningful local-store inventory differences**, rather than assuming a completely uniform national assortment at every point of sale.

It does **not** prove who owns inventory for each online SKU or how revenue/margin is allocated between central and local entities. Those remain **TO VERIFY**.
