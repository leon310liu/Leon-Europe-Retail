## 1. Why this matters

Conforama Spain should not be treated only as a furniture retailer for consumer-electronics channel mapping. Its current Spanish website explicitly includes **electrodomésticos**, image/sound products and a marketplace that expands into new categories, including **small appliances**.

For 001 AI / high-speed hair-dryer research, Conforama is relevant because current product pages show high-speed digital hair dryers from Cecotec and other sensor-equipped hair-dryer products being sold through third-party marketplace sellers, including a **Cecotec Official Store** seller identity.

This makes Conforama a distinct potential route for online market entry even when there is no evidence of Conforama central 1P purchasing.

## 8. Research model — INFERENCE, not official procurement chart

Based on Conforama's published marketplace mechanics, a useful research model is:

**Brand / Distributor / Marketplace Seller → Conforama Marketplace → Consumer**

For brands using their own official seller identity, a second working form is:

**Brand Official Store → Conforama Marketplace → Consumer**

This is a research abstraction based on the seller roles shown publicly. It is **not proof of the legal contracting entity, commission structure, warehouse ownership or invoice flow**.

A separate 1P route may exist for other products:

**Supplier → Conforama 1P → Consumer / Store**

but no public evidence found in this run proves that the cited hair-dryer SKUs follow this route.
