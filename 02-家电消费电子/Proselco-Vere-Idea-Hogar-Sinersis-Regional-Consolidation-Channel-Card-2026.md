## Why this matters
In July 2026, Proselco acquired a majority stake in Vere 85, S.A. and 100% of Idea Hogar Electrodomésticos, S.L. The transaction consolidates three regional Sinersis members under a broader operating perimeter while Vere and Idea Hogar continue their local activity.

This changes how the regional purchasing/logistics map inside Sinersis should be modeled. Proselco is no longer just one isolated regional platform: it now controls or fully owns two additional regional operators with meaningful store networks and a complementary kitchen-furniture specialist channel.

## Public facts

### 1. Transaction structure
- Proselco is a member company of Sinersis.
- Proselco acquired a **majority shareholding in Vere 85, S.A.**
- Proselco acquired **100% of Idea Hogar Electrodomésticos, S.L.**
- The Vere transaction was formalized on **23 July 2026**.
- The Idea Hogar transaction was formalized on **30 July 2026**.
- Both Vere and Idea Hogar were already Sinersis members before the transactions.

Sources:
- Electromarket, 2026-09-02: https://www.electromarket.com/noticia/34140/proselco-adquiere-vere-e-idea-hogar-y-refuerza-su-presencia-en-el-merc.html
- Alimarket Electro, 2026-09-03: https://www.alimarket.es/electro/noticia/430566/proselco-refuerza-su-peso-en-el-mercado-con-la-compra-de-idea-hogar-y-vere-85

### 2. Vere 85 regional footprint
- Main activity areas: **Castilla-La Mancha and Extremadura**.
- Retail banner: **Tien21**.
- Publicly disclosed network: **9 company-owned stores + more than 80 associated stores**.
- Sinersis still maintains Vere as an associated regional operator in its public structure.

Sources:
- Electromarket, 2026-09-02.
- Sinersis — Vere profile: https://www.sinersis.es/asociado/vere/

### 3. Idea Hogar regional footprint
- Main activity areas: **Castilla y León, Galicia, Asturias, Cantabria and País Vasco**.
- Retail banner: **Euronics**.
- Publicly disclosed network: **4 company-owned stores + more than 60 associated stores**.
- Complementary specialist channel: **more than 500 kitchen-furniture customers (mueblistas de cocina)**.

Source:
- Electromarket, 2026-09-02.

### 4. Integration model announced publicly
The public transaction announcement states that the three companies plan to:
- progressively integrate information systems and management tools;
- coordinate commercial and marketing strategies;
- improve operating efficiency;
- develop a more efficient logistics model;
- share capabilities and reduce duplication where joint management adds value;
- keep Vere and Idea Hogar operating with proximity to their local stores and markets.

The announcement also specifically identifies the **specialized kitchen-furniture channel** as a complementary channel with additional growth potential.

Source:
- Electromarket, 2026-09-02.

## What is fact vs inference
### Public fact
- Proselco acquired majority control of Vere and full ownership of Idea Hogar.
- Vere and Idea Hogar retain local activity.
- Commercial, marketing, IT and logistics coordination is planned.
- Vere has 9 owned + >80 associated Tien21 stores.
- Idea Hogar has 4 owned + >60 associated Euronics stores.
- Idea Hogar also serves >500 kitchen-furniture specialist customers.

### Reasonable inference — NOT a confirmed fact
- Proselco's effective negotiating scale with suppliers is likely to increase after the consolidation.
- Some categories may increasingly be coordinated across the three companies.
- The kitchen-furniture customer base could be relevant to built-in appliances and adjacent home-electronics categories.

These points must remain labeled as inference until supplier-side or company-side evidence confirms actual purchasing authority and category scope.
