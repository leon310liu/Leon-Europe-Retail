## 1. Channel identity

MediaMarkt España (MediaMarktSaturn, S.A.U.) is a national omnichannel consumer-electronics retailer. In its current 2026 corporate press material, MediaMarkt states that it operates 112 establishments across all Spanish autonomous communities, plus its online store and app, with around 7,000 technology specialists in Spain.

Official FY2025 figures disclosed by MediaMarkt: more than €2.75bn sales, >7% annual growth, and market share close to 22%. The company states that digital represented 16% of sales (+1.4pp vs 2024) and that its Marketplace is among the top 10 marketplaces in Spain according to the Lengow Barometer 2025.

MediaMarkt also states that its main Spanish logistics/service hub in Pinto (Madrid) exceeds 70,000 m². In 2025, 28% of deliveries used low-impact vehicles and last-mile zero-emission delivery was active in more than 30 Spanish cities; Sevilla and León Urban Hubs were planned to join the network in 2026.

## 2. Marketplace seller-entry rules — PUBLIC FACT

MediaMarkt's official Marketplace seller-registration page currently publishes the following minimum participation requirements:

- registered office in the EU;
- EU bank account;
- EU VAT registration;
- Spanish-language telephone customer-service line;
- direct shipping from the EU;
- EU returns address;
- EAN / GTIN for products;
- compliance with MediaMarkt content guidelines.

The registration form also asks for company name, store name, online assortment URL, main catalogue categories, and number of SKUs. MediaMarkt states that it validates the application before contacting the potential seller.

Source:
- MediaMarkt Marketplace registration: https://www.mediamarkt.es/es/about-us/marketplace-contact/marketplace-contact

### What this means for a China-origin supplier — ANALYTICAL INFERENCE

A China-only entity shipping consumer orders directly from China would not satisfy the currently published seller requirements because MediaMarkt explicitly requires EU establishment/banking/VAT, EU dispatch and an EU returns address. A China-origin brand therefore needs an EU-compliant operating structure or EU-based seller/distributor to use this route.

This is an inference from the published entry rules, NOT Leon View.

## 5. 1P vs Marketplace distinction — DATA-GOVERNANCE RULE

MediaMarkt product pages explicitly identify Marketplace offers with wording such as `Vendido y enviado por [seller]`. Therefore the knowledge base must NOT classify a MediaMarkt-listed product as MediaMarkt 1P merely because the SKU appears on mediamarkt.es.

For current 001 research, classify evidence as:

- **Marketplace confirmed**: an external `Vendido y enviado por ...` seller is displayed;
- **1P confirmed**: only when the page or another authoritative MediaMarkt source explicitly establishes MediaMarkt as seller / contracting party;
- **1P probable / TO VERIFY**: where the product has store pickup and no third-party seller is shown, but explicit seller evidence is absent from the captured page.

This is a data-governance rule to prevent false channel attribution; it is not Leon View.

## 7. Channel structure — ANALYTICAL INFERENCE

Public evidence supports at least two distinct MediaMarkt Spain entry paths:

**Route A — 1P retail (must be separately verified per SKU/brand)**
Brand / authorised supplier → MediaMarkt purchasing → MediaMarkt omnichannel stock → stores + mediamarkt.es + app → consumer

**Route B — Marketplace (directly verified)**
EU-compliant brand / distributor / seller → MediaMarkt Marketplace → mediamarkt.es → consumer

For Marketplace, the external seller can remain seller/fulfiller, as demonstrated by Aigotech, MORELE.NET, Digital Bay ES, SDA EUROPE SLU and DAM Electronics.

These structures are analytical inferences from public operating evidence, NOT Leon View.
