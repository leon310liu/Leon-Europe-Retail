## 2. Public facts

### 2.1 Faire scale and positioning

Faire states that it is a technology platform for wholesale connecting brands and retailers. Its current public Spain-facing pages state:

- 100,000 brands sell on Faire.
- Retailers use the platform across 70,000 cities.
- More than 10 million new commercial relationships have been created through the platform.
- It positions itself as a sourcing platform for retailers and supports categories including Beauty & Wellness.

Sources:
- https://www.faire.com/es/about
- https://www.faire.com/es/
- https://www.faire.com/es/brands

### 2.2 Retailer purchasing mechanics

Faire's Spain-facing retailer page publicly states:

- Thousands of brands offer low minimum orders or no minimum order.
- Eligible retailers can receive 60-day payment terms without interest.
- Faire offers free returns on the first order placed with each brand, subject to its terms.

Faire's Help Center further states that eligible first orders can generally be returned within a defined return window, with the applicable window displayed in the retailer portal.

Sources:
- https://www.faire.com/es/
- https://www.faire.com/es/support

### 2.3 Large-retailer route

Faire has a separate 'Faire for large retailers' proposition. It publicly states that large retailers can use a single Faire relationship to discover emerging brands, test smaller orders and centralise some operational/documentation processes across participating brands.

Source:
- https://www.faire.com/es/large-retailers

### 2.4 Brand-side economics outside North America

Faire's Help Center states that for brands headquartered outside North America, marketplace-discovered repeat orders carry a standard 15% commission, while the first order from a newly discovered customer can carry an additional one-time 10% new-customer fee, for a total of 25% on that first marketplace-discovered order. Faire Direct economics differ and should be evaluated separately.

Source:
- https://www.faire.com/es/support/articles/360040446591

## 5. Why this matters for 001

Public evidence confirms that a 110k RPM BLDC professional dryer is not only present in B2C retail and professional beauty distribution in Spain, but is also available through a B2B wholesale marketplace used by retailers.

This creates a distinct entry route from traditional Spanish buying groups:
