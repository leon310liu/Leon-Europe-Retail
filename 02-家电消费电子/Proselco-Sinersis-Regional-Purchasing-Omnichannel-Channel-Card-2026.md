## 2. Public facts — organizational position

### 2.1 Proselco is a Sinersis regional platform serving proximity retail

Sinersis identifies Proselco as a member of Sinersis and Euronics International and describes its role as providing integral service to associated stores.

Proselco's own current website describes itself as a specialist in developing branded proximity retail and states that it operates 246 points of sale, all specialized in household appliances and identified with one of its retail banners.

Sources:
- Sinersis — Proselco associate profile: https://www.sinersis.es/asociado/proselco/
- Proselco official site: https://www.proselco.com/

## 3. Public facts — logistics and assortment scale

### 3.1 12,000 m² warehouse with radio-frequency management

Proselco states that its logistics operation includes a 12,000 m² warehouse managed by an intelligent radio-frequency system and designed specifically for appliance logistics.

The company also states that associated stores in its area can be served within 48 hours and that orders can be placed as late as 22:00.

Source:
- Proselco official site: https://www.proselco.com/

### 3.2 146 brands and 15,000 rotating references

Proselco explicitly states under its Purchasing section that it works with 146 brands and 15,000 rotating references, supported by an agile and dynamic purchasing department.

It describes the objective of this purchasing department as obtaining better conditions, services and promotional actions for associated stores.

Source:
- Proselco official site: https://www.proselco.com/

This is a material public fact for route-to-market modelling because it confirms that Proselco performs an active purchasing function rather than functioning only as a logistics or branding layer.

## 4. Public facts — omnichannel / ecommerce operating model

### 4.1 Proselco states that ecommerce sales benefit its physical stores

Proselco's official website states: sales obtained through its sales websites are passed through / attributed for the direct benefit of its stores ("las ventas obtenidas a través de nuestras webs de venta son repercutidas a nuestras tiendas").

The same site describes online tools and physical stores as part of a single omnichannel model.

Source:
- Proselco official site: https://www.proselco.com/

This is important because it shows that ecommerce within this regional buying-group ecosystem is designed to preserve value for affiliated physical retailers rather than operate purely as an independent central ecommerce channel.

## 7. AI Analysis — not Leon View

1. Proselco should be modelled as a regional commercial platform with real purchasing, assortment, logistics and omnichannel functions, not merely as a warehouse or a banner administrator.
2. The public statement that web sales are attributed back to stores is important for understanding incentives inside Sinersis-style proximity retail: ecommerce and local store economics are intentionally linked.
3. For project 001, Proselco is a stronger target than an isolated independent appliance store because a successful listing or pilot could theoretically access a large regional store/logistics network; however the final authority for new PAE listings is not publicly established.
4. The combination of Sinersis national framework negotiation and Proselco's own purchasing department suggests a multi-level procurement process. Public sources do not establish which level has final authority for a new hair-dryer brand or SKU.
5. The `proselco-21` / `proselco-e` product metadata is useful operational evidence of regional-platform integration with national ecommerce, but should not be overinterpreted as proof of inventory ownership or seller-of-record status.

## 8. Data quality / conflict checks

### 8.1 Sinersis network-size inconsistency remains open

Sinersis' current homepage states 17 regional associated platforms, while its Associates / About pages still contain text referring to 16 associates. The Associates page also states that 19 warehouses coordinate procurement and logistics nationally.

Therefore:
- `17 associated platforms` = current homepage / current platform listing signal.
- `16 associates` = stale or inconsistent official-page text, not safe as a single definitive current fact.
- `19 warehouses` = current official Associates-page statement, but should remain source-dated because the site contains internal count inconsistencies.

Sources:
- https://www.sinersis.es/
- https://www.sinersis.es/asociados/
- https://www.sinersis.es/sobre-nosotros/

### 8.2 Historical network figures should not overwrite current Proselco figures

Older Sinersis material and third-party articles may use different national network counts. For this card, Proselco's current own-site figure of 246 points of sale is retained as the current company-published number.
