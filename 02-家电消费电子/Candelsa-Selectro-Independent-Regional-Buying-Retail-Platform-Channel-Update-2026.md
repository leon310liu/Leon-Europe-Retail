# Candelsa / Selectro — Independent Regional Buying & Retail Platform — Channel Update 2026

- **Knowledge type:** Public fact + explicitly labelled inference + verification backlog
- **Last verified:** 2026-09-10
- **Scope:** Spain appliance / consumer-electronics specialist distribution
- **Leon View:** **NONE ADDED OR MODIFIED**

## 1. Executive update

Candelsa should no longer be represented as a current Sinersis regional platform. Public evidence shows that Sinersis ended Candelsa's membership in May 2025; in January 2026 trade media reported that Candelsa created the **Selectro Electrodomèstics** identity for its own stores, which had previously operated under Tien21 and Euronics identities. Candelsa itself continues to describe the company as an independent purchasing centre for appliances, consumer electronics and new technologies.

This update therefore separates **historical affiliation** from **current channel status**.

---

## 2. PUBLIC FACTS

### 2.1 Candelsa identity and purchasing role

Candelsa's official corporate site states that Electrodomèstics Candelsa, S.A. was founded in 1972 and is a **central de compras** for appliances, consumer electronics and new technologies.

The company states that it serves retailers across **7 autonomous communities and more than 15 provinces** and reports sales volume of **more than €70 million**.

Candelsa also states that its purchasing scale enables it to negotiate competitive buying prices.

**Source:** Candelsa official company page, verified 2026-09-10  
https://www.candelsa.com/la-empresa/

### 2.2 Logistics / fulfilment model

Candelsa's official site states that its objective is to supply both stores and, where applicable, **directly to the end consumer**, reducing the need for unnecessary inventory at the retail point of sale.

Its current home page lists its area of operation as **Cataluña, Baleares, Aragón, Soria, La Rioja and Navarra**, and states that its warehouse operates 24/7.

**Sources:**  
https://www.candelsa.com/la-empresa/  
https://www.candelsa.com/

### 2.3 Separation from Sinersis

Trade publication Market Vision reported on **16 May 2025** that Sinersis had removed Candelsa as a member. A later June 2025 report described Sinersis as requiring Candelsa to cease use of the Tien21, Euronics and Confort banners, while allowing affected stores a transitional arrangement.

**Sources:**  
https://marketvision.es/cms/index.php/en-breve/negocios-btc/actualidad-abierta-negocios/negocios-panorama/sinersis-expulsa-a-candelsa-que-se-queda-sin-euronics-y-tien-21  
https://marketvision.es/cms/index.php/en-breve/negocios-btc/actualidad-abierta-negocios/negocios-panorama/sinersis-exige-a-candelsa-el-cese-en-el-uso-de-euronics-tien-21-y-confort-pero-lo-permitira-a-las-tiendas-transitoriamente

### 2.4 Selectro becomes Candelsa's own-store identity

Alimarket reported on **9 January 2026** that Candelsa created the **Selectro Electrodomèstics** identity for its own stores, which had previously been identified as Tien21 and Euronics. The same report states that its Mirò-owned-store line had disappeared from specialist distribution.

**Source:** Alimarket, 2026-01-09  
https://www.alimarket.es/electro/noticia/419403/selectro-electrodomestics-es-la-nueva-marca-de-candelsa

### 2.5 Current Sinersis network check

Sinersis' current associates page, verified 2026-09-10, does **not list Candelsa** among its regional associates. It currently states that Sinersis coordinates **16 associated platforms**, with **19 warehouses** supporting more than **1,500 points of sale**.

Important data-governance note: the same current Sinersis page visibly lists 17 named associate entries while its headline/body says 16. This is an internal inconsistency in the public source and must not be silently normalised.

**Source:** Sinersis official associates page  
https://www.sinersis.es/asociados/

In April 2024, Alimarket described Candelsa as a Sinersis platform operating mainly with Tien21 and Euronics banners. This is valid as a **historical 2024 fact**, but it became stale after the 2025 break with Sinersis and the 2026 Selectro transition.

**Source:** Alimarket, 2024-04-11

## 4. CHANNEL STRUCTURE

### Public-fact-supported entities

- Supplier / brand
- Candelsa as purchasing centre and logistics operator
- Selectro as Candelsa's own-store identity (reported January 2026)
- Retail / end consumer fulfilment

### INFERENCE — NOT PUBLICLY CONFIRMED AS A FORMAL PROCUREMENT FLOW

A practical current channel model for research purposes is:

**Brand / supplier → Candelsa purchasing & logistics layer → Selectro / Candelsa-associated retail layer → consumer**

This is an **inference from the public operating roles above**, not a disclosed Candelsa procurement org chart and not a Leon View.

Do **not** infer that:

- one central listing automatically covers all Candelsa-affiliated stores;
- Selectro equals the full Candelsa affiliated-store network;
- Candelsa currently has any specific AI / high-speed hair-dryer listing;
- Candelsa accepts direct supply from every overseas or Chinese brand.

## 7. DATA-GOVERNANCE FLAGS

### Stale mapping corrected

**OLD / HISTORICAL:** Candelsa → Sinersis → Tien21 / Euronics  
**CURRENT EVIDENCE:** Candelsa is no longer listed by Sinersis; Candelsa remains a purchasing centre; Selectro is reported as its new own-store identity.

### Website legacy-content warning

Candelsa's site still contains older pages describing Tien21 stores. These pages must be date-stamped as historical/legacy content and must not override the 2025–2026 separation evidence.

### Sinersis count inconsistency

The current Sinersis associates page says **16 associates** but visibly presents **17 named entries**. Store/platform counts should therefore always retain source date and source wording rather than being mechanically reconciled.
