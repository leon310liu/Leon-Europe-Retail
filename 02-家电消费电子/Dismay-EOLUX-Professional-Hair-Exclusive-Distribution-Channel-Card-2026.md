## 1. Entity

## 2. Verified public facts

### 2.1 Exclusive EOLUX distribution

EOLUX officially announced in 2026 that Dismay Hair & Beauty became its **official and exclusive distributor for Spain and Andorra**.

This is corroborated by BeautyMarket, which also describes the agreement as official and exclusive distribution for Spain and Andorra.

### 2.2 Direct 001 product relevance

Dismay currently lists EOLUX professional ionic brushless hair dryers.

Verified example:
- EOLUX EX04 / Ref. 47620
- Motor speed: 110,000 rpm
- Professional positioning
- Weight: 404 g
- Brushless / ionic positioning
- Status observed: in stock

Dismay also lists EOLUX EX02 1500W brushless ionic dryer.

Interempresas reported on 2026-05-22 that the EOLUX Professional Ionic Multi-Styler distributed by Dismay Hair & Beauty uses a **110,000 rpm digital motor** and 1875W power.

### 2.3 Downstream customer/channel role

BeautyMarket's company directory describes Dismay Hair & Beauty as a wholesaler of hairdressing and cosmetics products and says it distributes products and accessories to **wholesalers and specialist stores**.

This supports treating Dismay as a distribution-layer node rather than merely a consumer-facing retailer.

### 2.4 Exclusive-brand distribution model is not new for Dismay

Historical industry coverage shows Dismay has operated exclusive distribution relationships with professional beauty brands before, including Trendyhair (2018) and Joico (2020). These are historical facts and should not be assumed to represent current exclusivity unless separately reverified.

## 3. Channel structure inference

**Inference based on public facts — not Leon View:**

Brand / manufacturer → exclusive professional distributor (Dismay) → professional wholesalers / specialist hair-beauty stores / professional salon ecosystem

The exact split between Dismay's direct salon sales, specialist-retail sales, sub-wholesale sales and consumer sales is not publicly verified here.

## 4. 001 AI Hair Dryer implications — factual relevance only

The following are factual observations, not recommendations:

- A 110,000 rpm professional hair tool is already being distributed in Spain through an exclusive professional hair-beauty distributor model.
- Therefore, the Spanish 110k-rpm competitive landscape is not limited to mass retail, appliance chains, marketplaces or direct-to-consumer channels.
- Dismay provides a concrete benchmark for how a premium/professional high-speed dryer brand can use a specialized distributor to enter Spain.
