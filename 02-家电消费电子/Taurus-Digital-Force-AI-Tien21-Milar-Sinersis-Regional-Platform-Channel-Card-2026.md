## 1. Why this channel matters for 001 AI Hair Dryer

Taurus currently sells a product explicitly named **DIGITAL FORCE AI** in Spain. Taurus' own Spanish product page states that the dryer uses a high-speed digital motor and an **AI Scalp Protection Mode** with a dual intelligent sensor that detects both temperature and distance to the scalp and automatically adjusts heat.

The same model is currently listed by both **Tien21** and **Milar**, two major retail banners within the Sinersis ecosystem. This gives direct public evidence that a distance-sensing, AI-labelled high-speed hair dryer is already accepted into Spain's traditional appliance buying-group / regional-platform channel, not only into marketplaces or premium beauty retailers.

## 7. INFERENCE — what the channel structure suggests for 001

**INFERENCE (not Leon View):** the current public evidence supports a working structure broadly like:

`Brand / supplier → Sinersis commercial framework and/or regional associated platform → regional stock / product feed → Tien21 or Milar ecommerce + associated stores → consumer`

For Taurus DIGITAL FORCE AI specifically, Tien21's indexed feed visibly associates the SKU with **GRUDESA and SACSE-21**, demonstrating at least two regional-platform nodes connected to the live assortment.

However, the evidence does **not** establish whether:
- Taurus negotiated centrally with Sinersis;
- GRUDESA and SACSE independently selected the product;
- a separate national distributor supplies the regional platforms;
- Tien21/Milar central ecommerce can override regional ranging;
- the product is physically stocked in every associated store.
