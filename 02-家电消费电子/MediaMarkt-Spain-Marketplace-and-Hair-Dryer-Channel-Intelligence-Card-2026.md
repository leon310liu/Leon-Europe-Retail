## 2. Public facts — channel structure

### 2.1 MediaMarkt Spain operates both first-party retail and a third-party Marketplace

MediaMarkt Spain has an official Marketplace seller-registration page. The page states that applicants are reviewed before being accepted as possible Marketplace sellers.

Official source:
- MediaMarkt Spain — Marketplace seller registration: https://www.mediamarkt.es/es/about-us/marketplace-contact

### 2.2 Public Marketplace entry requirements

MediaMarkt Spain currently lists the following requirements for Marketplace sellers:

- registered office and bank account in the EU;
- EU VAT registration;
- Spanish-language telephone customer service line;
- direct shipping from the EU;
- EU returns address;
- EAN / GTIN product identifiers;
- compliance with MediaMarkt content guidelines.

These requirements are directly stated by MediaMarkt Spain and should be treated as verified public entry conditions for the Marketplace route.

Source:
- https://www.mediamarkt.es/es/about-us/marketplace-contact

### 2.3 Marketplace seller keeps a direct customer-service / warranty role

MediaMarkt Spain's customer-help pages state that customers with Marketplace order questions can contact the Marketplace seller directly. For manufacturer-warranty claims on Marketplace products, MediaMarkt instructs the customer to contact the seller, and the seller manages the repair process.

This is an important distinction from MediaMarkt first-party retail: Marketplace presence does not mean MediaMarkt assumes the same commercial and after-sales role as for first-party inventory.

Sources:
- Marketplace warranty: https://te-ayudamos.mediamarkt.es/app/answers/detail/a_id/19101/
- Contacting Marketplace seller: https://te-ayudamos.mediamarkt.es/app/answers/detail/a_id/18458/

## 4. Public facts — physical network scale: data-quality warning

MediaMarkt Spain's official “Quiénes somos” page currently states:

- 110 physical stores plus online store;
- 7,000 employees;
- EUR 2.304 billion Spain revenue.

However, the same page explicitly anchors these figures to fiscal year 2021/22. They must therefore NOT be treated as a 2026 audited store-count or revenue figure.

Source:
- https://www.mediamarkt.es/es/service/quienes-somos

Data-governance rule:
- `110 stores`, `7,000 employees`, and `EUR 2.304bn` may be retained only as historical 2021/22 official baseline data unless a newer official Spain-specific figure is found.

## 5. Channel model for project 001 — AI Analysis, not Leon View

### Route A — MediaMarkt Marketplace

Brand / EU seller entity → MediaMarkt Marketplace → end consumer

Public evidence supports this as a real route if the seller satisfies the EU entity / VAT / logistics / customer-service / EAN requirements above.

Marketplace seller responsibilities remain material, especially Spanish customer service, EU returns and warranty handling.

### Route B — MediaMarkt first-party retail procurement

Brand / importer / distributor → MediaMarkt retail procurement → MediaMarkt inventory / stores / online → end consumer

This route is structurally different from Marketplace. The public sources reviewed in this run do not disclose the buyer contact, onboarding procedure, listing fee, margin, payment terms, MOQ, launch test mechanism, central-vs-local buying authority or store-rollout rules for first-party hair-dryer procurement.

Therefore those items remain unverified.

## 6. Why this matters for 001 — AI Analysis, not Leon View

1. MediaMarkt.es is already a live Spanish retail environment for both conventional and intelligent/premium hair-care appliances.
2. Marketplace provides a publicly documented entry route that is materially easier to map than first-party retail procurement.
3. Marketplace acceptance should never be represented as equivalent to MediaMarkt first-party listing or physical-store distribution.
4. For 001, the operational barrier is not only product fit; the Marketplace route explicitly requires EU establishment/banking, EU VAT, EU fulfilment and returns, Spanish customer service, EAN/GTIN and compliant product content.
5. Current category pages show that price/value competition spans mainstream dryers through premium intelligent hair-care devices; therefore benchmarking should include both conventional hair dryers and premium intelligent products such as Shark and Dyson.
