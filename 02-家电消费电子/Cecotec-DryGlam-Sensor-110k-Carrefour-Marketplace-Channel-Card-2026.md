## Carrefour Marketplace public operating rules
Carrefour states that Marketplace products are sold by external third-party sellers selected by Carrefour, rather than bought directly from Carrefour.

Its seller recruitment page currently states:
- 170M+ annual visits.
- 8M loyal households.
- 2M products across categories.
- Seller workflow: create seller account with Marketplace team → upload catalogue → publish offers → prepare and ship orders.
- Seller receives money three times per month.
- No fixed membership fee.

These are public seller-facing rules. They do NOT prove that a new brand or seller is automatically accepted; Carrefour explicitly positions Marketplace sellers as selected external sellers.
