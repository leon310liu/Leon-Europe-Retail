# Dispel — Professional Hairdressing / Barber / Beauty B2B Distributor Channel Card (Spain, 2026)

> Status: Public-fact research card
> Last checked: 2026-09-02
> Scope: Spain / professional hairdressing, barbering, beauty and electrical appliances
> Leon View: **None added**

## 1. Channel classification

- Primary channel: Vertical professional channel — hairdressing / barbering / beauty
- Business model: Professional distributor + B2B professional pricing + online retail
- Geography: Spain, with professional orders shipped from Málaga
- Customer groups explicitly targeted by Dispel: salons, barber shops and beauty centres

## 2. Verified public facts

### Professional-distributor positioning

Dispel currently describes itself as a professional distributor of products for hairdressing, barbering, cosmetics and beauty, with its professional catalogue including electrical appliances.

Its current professional-products page states that it is a professional distributor based in Málaga and offers products/pricing aimed at salons, with 24–48h shipping across Peninsular Spain.

Source:
- https://dispelonline.com/es/categories/productos-peluqueria
- https://dispelonline.com/es

### Explicit B2B professional-price access mechanism

Dispel operates a documented professional-rate application process. The current public page states:

1. The professional creates an account.
2. The professional submits an application with business details.
3. Dispel reviews the request.
4. If approved, Dispel activates access to professional / distributor pricing.

The application asks for fields including business NIF, establishment address and products/brands of interest.

Dispel explicitly says these rates are for:
- hair salons,
- barber shops,
- beauty centres.

It also states that professional customers receive access to the professional-brand catalogue, product advice and shipment from Málaga.

Source:
- https://dispelonline.com/es/p/tarifas-para-profesionales

### Relevant hair-dryer / high-speed electrical assortment

Dispel currently sells WAD PROFESSIONAL BEAUTY professional hair dryers and labels itself an authorised official distributor on the product pages.

A directly relevant example is the WAD ELITE professional ionic dryer, currently described with:
- digital / brushless-type high-speed motor specification shown as “DBCL” on the page,
- 99,000 ± 5,000 rpm,
- 1,500–1,800 W,
- 310 g without cable,
- memory function,
- automatic cleaning,
- microfilter,
- diffuser + two nozzles,
- public retail price snapshot: from €208.95 VAT included.

This is not a 110,000 rpm product, but it is technically close to the high-speed BLDC / digital-motor architecture relevant to Product 001.

Dispel also currently lists WAD as one of the brands in its professional catalogue.

## 3. Entry-rule facts that are publicly visible

For professional customers, Dispel’s public entry process is unusually explicit compared with many Spanish professional-beauty distributors:

- Registration is required for professional pricing.
- Business verification is part of the application.
- NIF and establishment details are requested.
- Applicant can state specific brands/products of interest.
- Dispel reviews the application before professional pricing is activated.

These are **customer-side / buyer-side professional account rules**. They should **not** be confused with supplier onboarding rules for a new brand.

Supplier onboarding requirements, MOQ, payment terms, listing fees, exclusivity, rebates, inventory ownership and sell-in conditions are not publicly verified and remain TO VERIFY.

## 4. Channel-structure inference

> The following is an analytical inference from public facts, **not Leon View**.

Observed structure:

**Professional brand / supplier → Dispel professional distribution + online catalogue → verified professional accounts (salons / barbers / beauty centres) + retail buyers → end users**

This differs from:

- Carrefour / PcComponentes: broad consumer retail / marketplace routes;
- Sinersis regional platforms: appliance-retail buying-group architecture;
- The Beauty Corner: professional beauty seller + multi-marketplace architecture;
- Sunmarket Wellness: professional beauty equipment / showroom / service-heavy distribution.

Dispel is useful as a distinct sample of a **professional consumables + electrical-appliance distributor with explicit B2B account verification and distributor pricing**.
