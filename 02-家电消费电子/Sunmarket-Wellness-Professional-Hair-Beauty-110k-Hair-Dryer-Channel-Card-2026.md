## 3. Route-to-market structure

**Professional brand / authorised supply source → Sunmarket Wellness → professional e-commerce + Valencia showroom → hair salons / barbershops / aesthetic & wellness professionals**

Sunmarket also states that it serves customers across the EEA and uses GLS/Rhenus for deliveries, so its role is not limited to a single local Valencia store.

### Inference — not public fact

For 001, Sunmarket is structurally relevant as a **professional vertical distribution/e-commerce route**, because it already merchandises multiple 110k brushless professional hair dryers and explicitly serves salons/barbershops.

This does **not** prove that Sunmarket:
- directly imports WAD products from the manufacturer;
- is WAD's exclusive distributor;
- accepts new Chinese hair-dryer brands;
- accepts OEM/ODM/private-label proposals;
- buys inventory outright rather than via another distributor.

These remain `TO VERIFY`.
