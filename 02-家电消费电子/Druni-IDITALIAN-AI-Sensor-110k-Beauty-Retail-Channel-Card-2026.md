## 2. PUBLIC FACTS — current product evidence

### IDITALIAN Cyclone Multi Styler 5-in-1 on Druni

Druni's official product page currently publishes the following product claims:

- Product: IDITALIAN Cyclone Multi Styler, professional 5-in-1 hair dryer / multistyler.
- Published price snapshot: EUR 99.99.
- Motor: 110,000 rpm professional motor.
- `Ai-Sensor`: automatically detects the installed attachment and intelligently adjusts air temperature and air speed according to the attachment.
- `Hair Scalp Over Heat Protection`: protection against scalp overheating.
- Five included attachments: dryer, dryer nozzle, drying brush, straightening brush, and two Coanda-effect curlers.
- Three airflow speeds: 17 m/s, 24 m/s, 30 m/s.
- Dryer/curler temperature settings: 60°C, 80°C, 100°C.
- Straightening-brush temperature settings: 160°C, 180°C, 200°C.
- Published power: 1,200 W as dryer, 112 W as brush, 75 W as iron.
- The official product page and IDITALIAN brand page currently display the SKU as `No está disponible` (not available), despite retaining the EUR 99.99 price/listing.

### Druni × Arenal combination

Spain's CNMC states that it approved, with commitments, the creation of a joint venture combining the retail beauty and wellness businesses of Arenal and Druni. CNMC says the operation is jointly controlled by Modelo Continente Hipermercados, S.A. sucursal España (MCH) and the Casp family. The CNMC decision was authorized in first phase with commitments in June 2024.

Sources:
- CNMC case C/1456/24:
https://cnmc.es/expedientes/c145624
- CNMC press note dated 2024-06-28:
https://www.cnmc.es/novedad/compromisos-arenal-druni-20240628

Sonae's 2024 Integrated Annual Report states that the Druni/Arenal combination was completed in July 2024 and established a 50/50 partnership between MC and Druni's founding shareholders. The report states that Druni is fully consolidated by MC and Sonae due to governance rights in the agreement.
