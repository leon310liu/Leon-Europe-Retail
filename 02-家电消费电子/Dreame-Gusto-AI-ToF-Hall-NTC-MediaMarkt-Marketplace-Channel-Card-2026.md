Dreame Gusto is a directly relevant benchmark for 001 because the manufacturer publicly describes an AI-driven heat-control system combined with multiple sensors, rather than only using generic “smart temperature” language. ## 2. PUBLIC FACTS — product / technology

### 2.1 Manufacturer positioning

Dreame’s official product pages describe Gusto as using AI-driven / AI-adapted heat control for scalp protection.

Official Dreame sources:
- Dreame Serbia product page: https://sr.dreametech.com/product/dreame-gusto/
- Dreame Sweden product page: https://se.dreametech.com/products/gusto-hartork
- Dreame Global manual page, which includes ES among supported manual regions: https://global.dreametech.com/pages/gusto-high-speed-hair-dryer-user-manual

### 2.2 High-speed motor and airflow

Official disclosed specifications include:
- High-speed motor: 110,000 rpm
- Rated power: 2,000 W
- Airflow volume: 60 m³/h
- Airflow speed: 60 m/s
- Net weight: 517 g excluding cable

Source: Dreame official Gusto product pages above.

### 2.3 ToF distance sensing + AI heat adaptation

Dreame states that Gusto uses ToF (Time-of-Flight) sensor technology to detect the distance between the dryer and the head/scalp and dynamically adjust temperature in real time. The product description states that heat is automatically reduced as the dryer approaches the scalp.

This is stronger evidence than a generic “constant temperature” claim because the disclosed control input includes physical distance.

Source: https://sr.dreametech.com/product/dreame-gusto/

### 2.4 Hall sensor accessory recognition

Dreame states that a built-in Hall sensor automatically identifies an attached styling accessory and selects corresponding temperature / airflow parameters.

Source: https://sr.dreametech.com/product/dreame-gusto/

### 2.5 NTC measurement frequency

Dreame discloses an NTC thermistor measurement / checking frequency of 500 times per second.

Source: https://sr.dreametech.com/product/dreame-gusto/

### 2.6 Ion system and attachments

Official product information lists:
- 300 million negative ions/cm³
- 3 airflow speeds
- 4 heat settings
- styling nozzle
- diffuser
- wide-tooth comb
- oval brush

Source: https://sr.dreametech.com/product/dreame-gusto/
