## 2. Public facts — organizational position

### 2.1 UGESA is a regional purchasing central inside Sinersis

Sinersis' current official associate profile describes Unión Gallega de Electrodomésticos S.A. (UGESA) as a reference purchasing central for household appliances and consumer electronics in northwest Spain, operating since 1982.

The same official page states that UGESA currently has more than 38 socios and more than 150 asociados representing the Tien21 and Euronics banners in Galicia, Asturias and León.

Source:
- Sinersis — UGESA associate profile: https://www.sinersis.es/asociado/ugesa/

### 2.2 Sinersis coordinates national framework negotiations while regional associates coordinate procurement and logistics

Sinersis' current main site states that it coordinates the commercial interests of its regional associated platforms and negotiates framework and promotional agreements with suppliers.

Its current About page states that regional socios coordinate procurement and logistics in their areas of influence for the national store network.

Sources:
- Sinersis main site: https://www.sinersis.es/
- Sinersis About: https://www.sinersis.es/sobre-nosotros/

Important boundary:
- These public statements support a multi-layer purchasing structure.
- They do NOT prove which party has final listing authority for every small domestic appliance / personal-care SKU.

## 3. Public facts — national network context and current data conflicts

### 3.1 Sinersis official current network wording

Sinersis' current main page states more than 1,500 stores in Spain and shows current banner figures of 746 Tien21, 385 Milar and 380 Euronics stores.

The same page states that Sinersis coordinates 17 regional associated platforms.

Source:
- https://www.sinersis.es/

### 3.2 Sinersis About page uses a different associate count

Sinersis' current About page states 16 socios distributed across Spain coordinating procurement and logistics for more than 1,500 stores.

Source:
- https://www.sinersis.es/sobre-nosotros/

Data-quality rule:
- 17 associated platforms on the main page and 16 socios on the About page are retained as a current official-source conflict.
- Do not normalize them into one exact 2026 figure without clarification from Sinersis.

### 3.3 Current Euronics store-count pages are internally inconsistent

Euronics Spain's current “Quiénes somos” page states more than 400 stores in Spain and identifies a central-purchasing model.

A separate current Euronics “Confianza” page states more than 300 stores in Spain.

Meanwhile, Electromarket's 2025–2026 sector report gives 343 Euronics points of sale for 2025, down from 367 in 2024.

Sources:
- Euronics — Quiénes somos: https://www.euronics.es/quienes-somos.html
- Euronics — Confianza: https://www.euronics.es/confianza.html
- Electromarket sector report 2025–2026, page 45: https://www.electromarket.com/revista/378/45/

Data-quality rule:
- “400+”, “300+” and “343 in 2025” are source-specific figures with different timing / page maintenance.
- Do not present one as an exact verified September 2026 store count.

## 4. Public facts — direct relevance to project 001 AI hair dryer

### 4.1 Euronics currently sells a verified 110k brushless styling product

Euronics currently lists the Ufesa PRO GLAM SILK / PRO IONIC SILK styler with a digital brushless motor at 110,000 rpm, 1500 W, Coanda system, removable filter, ion generator, 3 temperatures and 2 speeds.

The Euronics product page also states free pickup in more than 400 Euronics stores.

Source:
- Euronics — Ufesa PRO GLAM SILK / PRO IONIC SILK: https://www.euronics.es/ufesa-pro-ionic-silk-moldeador.html

Important boundary:
- This proves 110k brushless hair-styling technology is present in the Euronics national ecommerce / store-linked ecosystem.
- It does NOT prove that all Euronics stores stock the product.
- It does NOT prove that UGESA purchased or listed this specific SKU for Galicia / Asturias / León.

### 4.2 Euronics product pages expose regional fulfillment identities

Current Euronics pages can show a specific fulfillment/store entity in the product data. Examples found during this verification include product pages showing identifiers such as `proselco-e` or other regional/store-linked fulfillment references.

This supports treating Euronics Spain as a distributed store-linked network rather than assuming every online SKU is fulfilled from one national inventory pool.

Examples:
- Imetec P7 3000 page: https://www.euronics.es/imetec-p7-3000-secador.html
- Shark SpeedStyle page: https://www.euronics.es/acer-speedstyle-rapidgloss-hd352eu-secador.html

Important boundary:
- These page identifiers are evidence of decentralized/store-linked fulfillment behavior.
- They do NOT by themselves disclose the legal inventory owner, supplier contract entity or national/regional buyer.

→ regional associated purchasing / logistics platform such as UGESA

## 6. AI Analysis — not Leon View

1. UGESA should be modeled as a real regional purchasing / distribution node inside the Sinersis ecosystem, not merely as a geographic label.
2. For project 001, Euronics' current sale of a 110,000 rpm brushless Ufesa hair-styling product establishes direct category-format compatibility at national Euronics ecosystem level.
3. Public Sinersis material supports a layered commercial model: national framework negotiation plus regional procurement/logistics execution.
4. The public evidence is insufficient to determine whether a new AI hair-dryer brand should approach Sinersis nationally, UGESA regionally, both sequentially, or through an existing importer/distributor.
5. Euronics store counts should not be treated as a single stable figure until the official 300+/400+ discrepancy and the 2025 industry count are reconciled.
