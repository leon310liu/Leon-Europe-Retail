## Status
- Updated: 2026-09-07
- Evidence type: PUBLIC FACTS + clearly labelled INFERENCE
- Leon View: **NONE ADDED**

## Channel role
**Public facts**
- Salonia Professional describes itself as an online seller of professional hairdressing products for professional customers in Spain.
- It states that it is an official distributor of multiple professional brands, explicitly naming Panasonic, Corioliss and IQ on its company page.
- Its public site offers professional advice, original spare parts, warranty support, 24–48h shipping and free shipping above €99.
- The business publicly merchandises a broad professional hair-dryer assortment rather than a single-brand offer.

**Inference (not public fact)**
- For the Spain GTM knowledge graph, Salonia is best modelled as a **vertical professional hair/beauty e-commerce + distributor/retailer node**, not as a mass-market electrical retail chain.
- No public evidence found in this run proves national wholesale coverage, a physical-store network, central-buying scale, or exclusive nationwide distribution rights for the brands it sells.

## Direct relevance to Product 001 AI / high-speed hair dryer
### 1. Corioliss Whisper
**Public facts**
- Salonia lists Corioliss Whisper with a BLDC motor at **110,000 rpm**, 1500–1800W, 330g, ionic generator, self-cleaning mode, 3 temperature/speed settings and professional 3m cable.
- Salonia explicitly states on this product page: **“somos distribuidores oficiales de Corioliss.”**

### 2. WAD Combo 6-in-1
**Public facts**
- Salonia lists WAD Combo 6-in-1 with a **110,000 rpm BLDC motor**, 1200W, 20/25/30 m/s airflow settings, PTC heating and six styling attachments.

### 3. Gama IQ1
**Public facts**
- Salonia lists Gama IQ1 with a **110,000 rpm brushless motor**, 2000W, LED display, 4 temperatures, 3 speeds, removable filter, memory function and 294g weight.
- The product page states Salonia is an **official distributor of Gama**.

### 4. Additional digital/brushless assortment
**Public facts**
- Salonia also lists other professional digital/brushless dryers such as Corioliss Mistral and Eurostil Penta.
- Salonia’s professional dryer category currently contains dozens of dryer listings and displays a broad multi-brand professional assortment.
