## 1. Why this channel matters

Sunmarket Wellness is a Spain-based specialist distributor serving professional beauty and wellness customers. It is relevant to the 001 AI / high-speed hair-dryer project because its current catalogue includes multiple professional 110,000 rpm BLDC hair dryers, while the company itself has a broader professional-customer, showroom, technical-service and financing infrastructure.

This makes it a useful example of a channel that sits between a hair-appliance brand and professional end users such as salons and beauty centres, rather than a mass retail chain or general marketplace.

## 4. Channel structure

### Public facts

The evidence confirms that Sunmarket:

1. acts as a specialist professional distributor / retailer in beauty, hairdressing and wellness equipment;
2. has a physical showroom and commercial-service infrastructure in Valencia;
3. serves professional customer groups such as salons, barbers, spas, beauty centres, gyms and hotels;
4. currently sells multiple 110,000 rpm BLDC professional hair dryers;
5. provides financing, technical advice and after-sales/service capabilities across its wider professional-equipment offer.

### Structural inference — NOT Leon View

A reasonable channel representation is:

**Hair-appliance / beauty brand → specialist professional distributor (Sunmarket Wellness) → salon / barber / beauty centre / spa / professional user (+ some consumer sales)**

This is a structural inference based on Sunmarket's stated customer groups and catalogue. It does **not** imply that every listed hair-dryer brand is covered by an exclusive or official distribution agreement.
